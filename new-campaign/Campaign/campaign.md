# The Soulwell Chamber

**A 3-Hour D&D 5e One-Shot for 5 Level 1 Players**

---

## Campaign Overview

**Party Size:** 5 players (one per role: Melee Striker, Ranged Striker, Spellcaster, Healer/Support, Skill Expert)  
**Starting Level:** 1  
**Session Length:** 3 hours (180 minutes)  
**In-Game Duration:** Approximately 3 hours

**Level Progression:**
- Start: Level 1
- After Puzzle 2 (Reflections): Advance to Level 2
- Before Puzzle 5 (Riddles): Advance to Level 3

---

## DM Introduction

Read the following to your players:

---

Your vision returns slowly—first light, then shape, then sound.

You find yourselves standing inside a perfectly circular stone chamber, its surface polished smooth as glass. The air is unnervingly still… almost *expectant*.

Four braziers stand evenly spaced around you, each burning with a steady blue-white flame. The fire gives off no heat. Instead, it pulses faintly, like a slowed heartbeat—its rhythm just slightly out of sync with your own.

At the center of the room lies a **large pentagram**, carved with flawless, almost obsessive precision. Five unfamiliar sigils are etched into the stone around it, but **only one** emits light: a soft, rhythmic **golden pulse**, rising and dimming like slow breathing.
The remaining four sigils sit dark and dormant, waiting their turn.

As you shift your weight, you notice something strange—
your footsteps echo half a heartbeat late, as if an invisible presence is walking just behind you.

The temperature of the chamber wavers subtly.
A passing flicker of fear cools the air; a moment of resolve warms it again.
The room is reacting to you.

There are **no doors, no seams, no windows**. Every inch of wall and ceiling is an unbroken surface. Yet when the brazier-light catches the stone just right, you see thousands of tiny inscriptions—etched by dozens of different hands.

Prayers.
Warnings.
Names.
All carved by those who stood here before you.

Then, as your eyes rise toward the ceiling, the stone above seems to distort—not breaking, but *bending* like a mirage. A shimmering haze ripples across its surface, vanishing as quickly as it appears.

```
A character who succeeds on a **DC 18 Arcana check** senses that the distortion is not an illusion of light, but a veil—thin, unstable, and threaded with powerful magic.

With success, they glimpse what the chamber is trying to conceal:

A **Soulwell**, suspended beyond the physical plane, its form a swirling construct of shadow and luminescence turning in impossible shapes.
From it, faint threads of shimmering essence descend like gossamer strands and **tether themselves to each of you**. You feel no pain, no drain… but your souls are undeniably connected to the thing above.
A constant, gentle pull—like a distant inhale.

*DM Note:*
With each puzzle completed, the magical veil weakens.
The DC to perceive the Soulwell **decreases by 2**, eventually allowing the truth of the room to become unmistakable even to the untrained eye.
```

And then, from the pentagram beneath your feet, you feel something answer.

A slow intake of air.
A subtle draw toward the glowing sigil.
As though the *floor itself* has just… breathed.

The chamber waits.
And whatever brought you here is far from finished with you.
