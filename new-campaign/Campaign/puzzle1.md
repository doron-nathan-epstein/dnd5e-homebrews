# **Puzzle 1: The Hall of Ordered Form**

## **Read or paraphrase the following to your players:**

The golden glow of the active sigil at the pentagram’s edge pulses once—
slow and deep—
like the chamber taking a deliberate breath.

Then the air around the pentagram begins to shimmer.

Light bends.
Shadows twist.
And from thin, swirling motes of bone-dust and reflected light, **five floating mirrors materialize**, each locking into place before a point of the pentagram.

They hover there, drifting ever so slightly, as though breathing with the room itself.
Each mirror is framed in an elaborate construction of bones—
**skulls, jawbones, clavicles, scapulae, humeri, ribs, spines, pelvises, femurs, kneecaps, tibias, fibulas, and toes**—
all woven into grotesque, ornamental arrangements.

Yet no two mirrors are the same.
Some frames spiral; others stack in impossible geometry.
Some seem chaotic.
Some strangely artistic.

And then, echoing from nowhere and everywhere at once, a **mysterious voice** fills the chamber:

> **“Head, shoulders, knees, and toes.
> Before the taller, you each must go.
>
> Reflect in height upon your visage,
> For in this order, on your souls I will pillage.
>
> For each of you a sigil assigned—
> Remember this for when on your souls I will dine.
>
> Get in line, hurry—chop chop.
> The clock waits for no one. Tick tock.”**

---

# **Puzzle Mechanics**

## **Step 1: Identifying the Correct Mirror**

The players must examine the five mirrors.

Although each mirror showcases all manner of skeletal pieces, **only one features the five key skeletal structures in true anatomical descending order**:

**Skull → Shoulders → Spine → Knees → Toes**

The others misplace, reverse, spiral, or break this sequence.

Players may identify the correct mirror with:

* **Investigation**, **Medicine**, or **Perception** check (DC 10)
* Or **Passive Perception 14**

No roll needed for players who simply figure it out.

**DM Note:** Randomly determine which of the five mirrors is correct for this run (roll 1d5, or simply pick one). The specific mirror doesn't matter mechanically—what matters is that players learn the anatomy order from identifying it.

---

## **Step 2: Height Order Lineup**

Once the correct mirror is found, players must **line up before it in height order**, shortest to tallest—
*“before the taller you each must go.”*

When a player stands in the **correct position**:

* Their matching sigil on the pentagram begins to **breathe with a soft golden glow**
* A **thin golden beam** rises vertically from the circular ring around that sigil
  (indicating correct placement)

When a player stands in the **wrong position**:

* All breathing glows cease
* All beams vanish
* The sigils flicker and fall dark again

Once the lineup is **perfect**:

* The **first sigil** stops breathing and transforms from gold into a **vivid emerald green**
* Its beam brightens sharply, then extinguishes
* The sigil remains a **solid emerald glow**, marking the task complete
* The five mirrors **melt away**, dissolving into drifting golden vapour that evaporates into the air
* Immediately, the **second sigil clockwise** around the pentagram awakens, beginning its own soft, golden, breathing pulse—the next trial is ready

---

# **Sigil Significance (DM-Only)**

Each player effectively receives their own sigil through this puzzle.
These sigils will later be crucial in the **final encounter**—either to weaken, bind, or entrap the BBEG.

The players **should not** learn this purpose yet.
