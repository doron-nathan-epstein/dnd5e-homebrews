# **Puzzle Room: The Hall of Shattered Selves**

*(Puzzle 2 – Triggered after Puzzle 1 completes and the second sigil begins its golden breathing pulse)*

---

## **Read or paraphrase the following to your players:**

As the first sigil settles into its steady emerald glow, the chamber grows still again.
The soft blue-white light of the braziers steadies, as though waiting for the room to breathe its next breath.

Then—
the golden vapors left behind from the dissolving mirrors drift upward, swirling together in lazy spirals above the pentagram.
At first they look like harmless mist.

But then they begin… **seeking.**

The vapors stretch toward each of you, dividing cleanly—one stream per person—circling you like curious serpents before pooling upon the floor directly in front of you.

The vapors thicken, condense, and rise.

A humanoid outline forms.

Then bone-white lines trace a skeleton made of thin, delicate lattices of light.

Then flesh fills in like a watercolor bleeding through paper.

Within seconds, standing before each of you is:

# **You.**

But not as you are now.
A lesser version. Simpler gear. Fewer scars. Unhardened eyes.
A body laced with faint golden cracks, as though held together by magic pressed too tightly.

They tilt their heads in perfect mimicry of your movements,
but always **half a heartbeat late**,
like reflections trapped behind time.

The second sigil on the pentagram pulses in slow golden breaths.

The chamber waits.

And your reflections… step forward.

# **Puzzle Mechanics**

## **Step 1: The Manifest Reflection (No Room Change)**

Nothing in the Soulwell Chamber shifts, moves, or reshapes.
The same smooth walls, the same four braziers, the same pentagram—unchanged.

The reflections simply **manifest**, birthed from the golden remains of Puzzle 1’s mirrors.

They stand in positions mirroring the players’ current places in the room.

They make no sound.

They wait for the players’ first move.

---

## **Combat Encounter: “Your Weaker Self”**

Each player faces a manifested copy of themselves at **Level 1**:

* Same race
* Same class
* Same subclass (if chosen at Level 1—otherwise: base class features only)
* Basic gear equivalent to a starting kit
* Hit points appropriate for a Level 1 version of that class
* Ability scores *match the player* (but HP and abilities scale to level 1)

### **Combat Behavior**

Your reflection:

* Always attempts to flank you if possible
* Copies your *style*, not your *specific abilities*
* Uses basic tactics, like a version of you before experience hardened your instincts
* Fights silently
* Moves exactly as you do—
  with a fractional delay
  like a mirror struggling to keep up

### **Optional Twist (Highly Recommended):**

If any player attempts to attack someone else’s reflection:

* That reflection mimics **that player** instead—
  shifting subtly at the moment of aggression
  as if it is “choosing” a new source to copy.

This keeps the fight personal, unsettling, and thematically sharp.

---

## **Shatter Condition**

When a reflection is reduced to 0 HP:

* It does **not** fall unconscious
* It stands perfectly still
* Its form begins to fracture like a mirror under pressure
* Cracks race through its body in straight lines of bright gold
* Then—
  **it shatters**, exploding into thousands of tiny golden shards
* The shards dissolve instantly into dust
* A faint **echo of the player’s own voice** (level 1 tone) whispers a single word:
  “Why?”
  or
  “Again?”
  or
  “Become.”

Each reflection’s death is a moment of eerie personal symbolism.

---

## **Puzzle Completion**

Once **all reflections are shattered**, the following occurs:

* The second sigil on the pentagram stops its soft golden breathing
* It ignites into a **vivid emerald green**, matching the first
* A thin beam of emerald light shoots upward from its ring to the ceiling
* The beam flickers out
* The sigil remains solid and steady

And then—

A fresh inhalation of the chamber.
A subtle pull from the Soulwell above.
A shift in the other sigils, as though the third is preparing to awaken.

The next challenge is ready to begin.

---

## **DM-Only Notes:**

### **Balance Suggestions**

If your party is strong:

* Reflections roll initiative with advantage
* Grant them a once-per-fight “Surge of Instinct”:
  A reflection can take one extra attack/action at ¼ health

If your party is struggling:

* Reduce reflection HP slightly
* Have reflections act second each round, always after the player they mirror
* Allow reflections to hesitate if the players attempt roleplay or introspection

### **Sigil Meaning**

Each reflection slain binds another portion of the players’ souls to the Soulwell’s growing ritual structure.
The players still have **no idea**, and should not yet.

## Level Progression

After this puzzle is complete, all players **advance to Level 2** before Puzzle 3 begins. Notify them: "You feel the chamber’s energy flow through you. You’ve learned much. You are Level 2."

Healing during the brief transition: Players can spend Hit Dice to recover health before Puzzle 3 begins.
