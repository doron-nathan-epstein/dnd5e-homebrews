# **Puzzle Room: The Hall of Elemental Remains**

*(Puzzle 3 – Triggered after Puzzle 2 completes and the third sigil begins its soft, golden breathing pulse)*

---

## **Read or paraphrase the following to your players:**

As the echoes of the shattered reflections fade, the chamber settles once more into its unnatural stillness.

The braziers flicker—once, twice—
then steady again, their blue-white flames dimming as though considering what comes next.

From the spots where each reflection died,
**five faint piles of golden dust remain**, sparkling like crushed starlight on the stone floor.

Then the dust… moves.

It draws inward, spiraling tightly, each pile pulling itself into a long, thin shape.
Bone materializes at the center of each swirl—clean, pale white, polished like ivory.

In seconds, **five small bones lie on the floor**, each one perfectly formed.
They hum faintly, resonating with quiet elemental energy.

Then, as the third sigil pulses brighter, the bones rise gently into the air.

The moment any of you reaches toward one—
the bone **changes**, reshaping smoothly like melting wax drawn upward:

A wooden shaft grows from its length.
Runes burn themselves along the grain.
The bone fuses into a core at the top.

Each bone becomes a **wand**.

Each wand glows with a faint aura—
five distinct colors, five distinct elements.

The chamber waits.

---

# **The Five Elemental Wands**

Each wand corresponds to a classic element and is imbued with a single cantrip.
Only the **correct** person can use each wand.

This is where the **order from Puzzle 1** matters again.

The order the players stood in (shortest → tallest) determines the order of elemental attunement.

Assign the wands to players in that same sequence:

1. **Spirit – Light** *(soft white glow)*
2. **Earth – Mold Earth** *(dark green rumble)*
3. **Fire – Fire Bolt** *(red-orange heat shimmer)*
4. **Water – Shape Water** *(cool blue ripple)*
5. **Air – Gust** *(pale silver swirl)*

When a player picks up *their* wand:

* It warms to their touch
* Its runes flare with color
* The magic settles naturally within their hand

When a player picks up a wand that is **not theirs**:

* The wand flickers uncertainly
* Sparks jump from the bone core
* The runes sputter
* The wand becomes inert in their grip
* **The cantrip cannot be cast**

Nothing explodes.
But the rejection is unmistakable.

---

# **Puzzle Mechanics**

## **Step 1 – Claiming the Wands**

The players must pick up the wands and determine which one aligns with which person.

A successful **Arcana (DC 12)** or **Insight (DC 14)** check reveals:

> “The wands refuse you unless you take the one meant for you.”

If they cannot figure it out immediately, the wands’ reactions will teach them.

---

## **Step 2 – Casting on the Sigils**

The five sigils on the pentagram (one emerald, four still dormant) each respond to their associated element.

When the correct player casts their cantrip on their corresponding sigil:

* The sigil pulses gold once
* A thin beam of gold shoots upward from its surrounding ring
* The beam flickers
* The sigil continues its golden breathing, awaiting the others

### **If the order is incorrect**

If a player casts:

* the wrong spell, **or**
* the right spell but not in sequence, **or**
* the right spell but with the wrong wand (if they swapped wands)

then:

* All sigils extinguish
* The third sigil resets
* The wands revert to inert bones
* The puzzle begins again

A soft whisper echoes through the chamber:

> “Order… order… order…”

---

## **Correct Sequence**

The sequence mirrors Puzzle 1’s height lineup:

1. **Spirit – Light**
2. **Earth – Mold Earth**
3. **Fire – Fire Bolt**
4. **Water – Shape Water**
5. **Air – Gust**

As each sigil is correctly activated, the next one begins to glow with the soft golden breathing pulse—inviting the next spell.

---

## **Puzzle Completion**

When the final spell is cast correctly:

* The fifth sigil flashes a brilliant gold beam
* All five beams ignite in unison for a single heartbeat
* Then explode upward into the ceiling like dissipating fireworks
* Each wand dissolves into gentle golden dust
* The dust rises and vanishes into the Soulwell above
* The third sigil turns solid emerald
* The room inhales once more

The **fourth sigil** begins its slow golden pulse.

The next challenge awakens.

---

## **DM-Only Notes**

* This puzzle reinforces the height-order theme.
* This puzzle subtly strengthens the tether between players and the Soulwell as their magic is channeled through it.
* The wands should not be usable outside the puzzle.
