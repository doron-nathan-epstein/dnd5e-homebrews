# **Puzzle Room: The Hall of Shifting Dust**

*(Puzzle 4 – Triggered when the fourth sigil begins its golden, breathing pulse)*

---

## **Read or paraphrase the following to your players:**

As the last traces of golden dust from the wands drift upward and vanish into the Soulwell, the chamber falls silent again—
a silence so heavy it feels like the air itself is waiting for something to happen.

Then it does.

The blue-white flames of the braziers gutter, shrinking inward until they flicker like dying candles.
The temperature drops sharply—dry, brittle, uncomfortable.

A faint *scrape* echoes across the stone floor.

Then another.

Then dozens more.

Above you, the ceiling—the same ceiling where the Soulwell swirls in its otherworldly haze—
begins to weep grains of sand.

Not fall.
Not pour.

**Weep.**

Individual grains trickle downward in thin, deliberate lines like tears.
Then the trickles become streams.
The streams become rivulets.
And the rivulets become a rain of sand.

The sand gathers on the floor, pulling itself inward with unnatural intent.

You watch as the pile splits into two distinct shapes—

One swirling and rising like a miniature desert storm…
The other flattening into a carpet of shifting, chittering motion.

The room exhales a low, grinding whisper:

> **“Endure… or be consumed.”**

The sand finishes its transformation.

---

# **The Foes Formed From Dust**

You now face two constructs born from the chamber’s will:

### **1. The Sandbound Guardian**

A **Small Sand Elemental**, humanoid only in silhouette—
its form constantly collapsing and rebuilding itself,
grains pouring like hourglass sand with each step.

### **2. The Devouring Swarm**

A **Swarm of Bone-Sand Scarabs**, tiny crystalline beetles made entirely of compressed dust.
Their bodies glow faintly with golden light, like sparks caught in amber.

Both creatures are **partially tethered to the Soulwell**.
They leave trails of drifting sand behind them as they move.

---

# **Puzzle Mechanics / Encounter Notes**

## **The Goal**

Survive.
There is no trick, no puzzle input—this challenge is purely **endurance and teamwork**.

---

## **Combat Stat Blocks**

**See enemy stat blocks for complete mechanical details:**
- `enemies/04-sandbound-guardian.md` — AC 14, HP 22, Slam attack, Sand Spray ability
- `enemies/04-scarab-swarm.md` — AC 12, HP 16, Bite attack, climbing ability

**Quick Reference:**
- **Sandbound Guardian:** Treat as Mephit-tier elemental. Slam attack (bludgeoning). Vulnerable to water (double damage). Resistant to fire and piercing.
- **Swarm of Scarabs:** Moves across floor and climbs on creatures. Piercing/slashing do half damage. Bludgeoning does normal damage and spreads swarm apart. Vulnerable to area effects.

---

# **Room Interactions**

During the fight:

* The braziers dim to **half-light**, creating long dramatic shadows
* The pentagram’s fourth sigil pulses faster as the battle continues, responding to violence
* Footsteps echo half a beat late (a reminder of the tethered souls)
* The sand moves unnaturally:

  * It avoids the braziers
  * It snakes along cracks
  * It pulls toward the sigils whenever blood hits the floor

---

# **Puzzle Completion**

When both creatures are defeated:

* All remaining sand shudders
* Then rises in swirling spirals into the air
* The Soulwell above pulses, inhaling the final grains

The fourth sigil:

* Stops its golden breathing
* Shifts into a brilliant **emerald green**
* A soft pulse of energy sweeps across the chamber like a shockwave
* The braziers return to their steady blue-white flame

Then, as always:

The **final sigil**, the fifth, begins its gentle golden glow.

Only one trial remains.
