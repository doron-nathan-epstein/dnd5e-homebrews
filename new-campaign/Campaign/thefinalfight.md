# ⭐ **Final Encounter: Kaelthys, the Shifting Riddle**

*The Guardian of the Soulwell*

This encounter begins **immediately after Puzzle 5** resolves and the final sigil turns emerald.

---

# **DM Read-Aloud: The Awakening of Kaelthys**

As the final answer leaves your lips, the emerald sigil at the center of the pentagram **erupts in brilliance**—a silent explosion of color that spreads through each carved line like lightning.

The chamber inhales.

The sands from the previous puzzle lift as though weightless, spiraling upward in shimmering currents. Scarab shells click and rattle as they rise, joining the dance of swirling golden grains. The air thickens, humming with power.

Then—
the swirling mass **folds inward**, compressing into a single molten point of light.

With a deafening *THRUM*, the point explodes outward into shape.

**A massive leonine form lands before you, shaking the chamber with the impact.**
Bronze wings flare open.
A mane of rippling, glowing script shifts around his head.
Eyes of quicksilver regard you with amused contempt.

A voice echoes not in your ears—
but **inside your minds**, layered a thousandfold:

> **“You have tugged at the threads of the Soulwell…
> And so at last, you reach its Keeper.”**

He smiles, sharp and ancient.

> **“I am Kaelthys — the Shifting Riddle.
> Guardian of names, devourer of answers,
> and judge of the clever.”**

Around him, the Soulwell above pulses faster, its tethers tightening around each of your souls.

> **“Five trials you’ve passed.
> Five sigils awakened.
> Five questions remain.”**

He rises, wings unfurling to fill half the chamber.

> **“Answer them…
> or be unmade.”**

Combat begins — but **with riddles intertwined**.

---

# ⭐ **Encounter Structure**

Kaelthys fights as an **Androsphinx (CR 11–13)** with spellcasting and legendary actions, plus **the Riddle Phase mechanic** below.

This fight has **three interwoven components**:

1. **Combat with Kaelthys**
2. **Riddle Interruptions (Legendary Actions)**
3. **Sigil Empowerment** based on how many riddles are solved

---

# ⭐ **Riddle Mechanic: The Five Final Questions**

At the end of each of Kaelthys’s turns, he uses his legendary action **Riddle Interruption** (cannot be countered or silenced).

He asks one of the five riddles. The party can answer as a free action.

The order of riddles matches the five earlier puzzle themes:

1. **Air – Easy**
2. **Water – Moderate**
3. **Earth – Challenging**
4. **Fire – Hard**
5. **Spirit – Very Hard**

---

# ⭐ **Riddle Effects**

When a riddle is asked:

* Each PC must succeed a **DC 16 Intelligence save** or lose their next action, mesmerized.
* **BUT** if a player gives the correct answer aloud:

  * All PCs immediately succeed the save.
  * The sigil corresponding to that element **flashes emerald**.
  * Kaelthys takes **10 radiant damage** (per correct riddle).
  * PCs gain a temporary buff (listed below).

If the party answers incorrectly:

* Kaelthys heals **15 HP**
* The emotional “pulse” of the room shudders and deals **4 psychic damage** to all PCs
* That sigil remains dormant
* Kaelthys laughs, and gains advantage on his next attack

---

# ⭐ **The Five Final Riddles**

---

## **Riddle 1 – Air (Easy)**

> “I dance without legs and sing without a tongue.
> I move freely but leave nothing behind.
> What am I?”

**Answer:** *Wind / Air*

**Correct Answer Reward:**
All PCs gain **+10 ft movement** for 2 rounds.

---

## **Riddle 2 – Water (Moderate)**

> “I fall but never rise, I flow but never sleep.
> I can carve mountains or drown a city.
> What am I?”

**Answer:** *Water / River*

**Correct Answer Reward:**
PCs gain **resistance to Fire** for 2 rounds.

---

## **Riddle 3 – Earth (Challenging)**

> “I stand silent for centuries, holding secrets in my grasp.
> I can crumble kingdoms or shelter the meek.
> What am I?”

**Answer:** *Mountain / Earth*

**Correct Answer Reward:**
PCs gain **temporary HP equal to their level**.

---

## **Riddle 4 – Fire (Hard)**

> “I consume yet never hunger, I give warmth yet burn the unwary.
> I dance, I rage, I illuminate the dark.
> What am I?”

**Answer:** *Fire / Flame*

**Correct Answer Reward:**
All PCs’ next weapon attack or spell deals **+1d6 radiant damage**.

---

## ⭐ **Riddle 5 – Spirit (Very Hard)**

This replaces the old “Sphinx” riddle with something far stronger.

> “I travel light though I am carried always.
> I am spoken to summon, kept to hide, and lost to time.
> I can bind a promise and undo a vow;
> I can free a ghost or steal its face.
> What am I?”

**Answer:** *A Name / True Name*

**Correct Answer Reward (Major Power):**

* All soul-tethers flare bright white
* PCs gain **advantage on all rolls for 1 round**
* Kaelthys becomes **vulnerable to the next attack or spell**
* The final sigil burned into the pentagram erupts in emerald light

If this final riddle is answered correctly, Kaelthys snarls:

> **“You dare speak of names before *me*?”**

And the boss becomes **staggered for 1 round**, unable to use legendary actions.

---

# ⭐ **The Sigil Finale**

If the party answers **three or more riddles correctly**, then when Kaelthys reaches 0 HP:

> The five emerald sigils ignite at once.
> Beams of emerald light erupt upward, latching onto Kaelthys.
> His form contorts—shredded by the power he once commanded.
> The Soulwell above collapses inward with a deafening roar.

Kaelthys is banished, and the Soulwell loses control over their souls.

If fewer than three riddles were answered:

* Kaelthys’s death triggers a **partial collapse**
* A soulquake ripples through the chamber
* One PC takes a dangerous magical consequence (DM choice: exhaustion, lose 1 max HP, temporary curse, etc.)
* The Soulwell collapses chaotically

---

# ⭐ **Kaelthys’s Legendary Actions (Updated)**

Kaelthys retains Androsphinx legendary actions plus:

### **Riddle Interruption**

He asks a riddle. PCs must save or lose an action. Correct answer cancels the effect and damages him.

### **Soul Snap (New)**

Kaelthys snaps a tether.
One PC makes a DC 17 Wis save or takes **10 psychic damage** and becomes **frightened** for 1 round.

### **Sandstorm Blink (New)**

Kaelthys becomes sand, teleports 40 ft, and gains **half cover** until his next turn.

---

# ⭐ **Kaelthys’s Final Words (if defeated)**

As he dissolves into silver-gold motes:

> **“Even shattered… I am never truly gone.
> A riddle unanswered waits forever.”**

The motes spiral upward and vanish into the collapsing Soulwell.
