# Character Selection Guide

**For DMs and Players:** Pick one character from each role below. You'll have 5 total party members.

---

## How to Use This Guide

1. **Decide which role appeals to you:** Do you want to hit things? Cast spells? Heal? Support?
2. **Pick a character from that role:** Each role has 2–3 options with different flavors
3. **Review the character sheet:** Read their personality, abilities, and equipment
4. **You're ready to play!** No experience needed—all characters are beginner-friendly

## Tips for New Players

1. **Don't worry about optimization.** All characters are well-balanced. Pick whoever sounds fun.
2. **Ask questions.** Your DM is there to help you understand your abilities.
3. **Trust the character sheet.** Everything you need to play is listed clearly.
4. **Work together.** This campaign works best as a team. Communicate with other players about what you're doing.

---

## Tips for DMs

1. **Print these sheets** and hand them to players as they arrive.
2. **Let players read for 5 minutes** to pick a character and role.
3. **Each character is self-contained.** No character requires another to function.
4. **Heights matter in Puzzle 1.** Whichever 5 you get, the height spread is good.
5. **All characters have roughly equal damage/healing output** at Level 1. Balance is built in.

---

## Notes on Balance

- **Melee Strikers (Role 1):** High HP and damage, lower AC depending on class
- **Ranged Strikers (Role 2):** Medium HP, high damage, medium AC
- **Spellcasters (Role 3):** Low HP, spells for versatility, low AC (watch them!)
- **Healers (Role 4):** Medium HP, healing, medium-to-high AC, good defense
- **Skill Expert (Role 5):** Medium HP, lots of skills, medium AC, inspiration support

**No role is "better" than another.** The party works best with all 5 roles represented.

---

## Level Progression

Characters start at **Level 1**.

- After **Puzzle 2 (reflection fight)**, all characters gain a **level** → **Level 2**
- Before **Puzzle 5 (riddles)**, all characters gain another **level** → **Level 3**

This progression is built into the campaign timing. The DM will announce level-ups at the appropriate moments.

---

## Spellcasting & Wands (Puzzle 3)

In **Puzzle 3**, characters receive magical **wands** that grant **cantrips** temporarily.

**Important:** Characters don't need to be spellcasters to use the wands. A **Barbarian can cast Fire Bolt** from the wand, for example. The wand handles the magic—just cast it like you would any other ability.

Wand cantrips available:
- Light (Spirit)
- Mold Earth (Earth)
- Fire Bolt (Fire)
- Shape Water (Water)
- Gust (Air)
