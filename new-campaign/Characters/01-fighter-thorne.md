# Thorne

**Race:** Human  
**Class:** Fighter  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'11"  

## Personality
A seasoned-looking mercenary with a pragmatic outlook. Thorne speaks little but acts decisively. He's seen enough of the world to know when to fight and when to listen.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 14 | +2 |
| DEX | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 11
- **Armor Class:** 16 (chain mail)
- **Initiative:** +1

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** STR +4, CON +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +1  
**Proficiencies:** All simple and martial weapons, all armor and shields

## Equipment

- Longsword (or Greataxe)
- Chain mail
- Shield
- Explorer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Fighting Style:** Duelist (when wielding a melee weapon and no shield, +2 to damage rolls)  
**Second Wind:** Can use a bonus action to regain 1d10 + 1 HP (once per rest)

## Notes

Thorne is straightforward and reliable. Good for new players—attack with weapon, use Second Wind to heal, and use Fighting Style bonus damage.
