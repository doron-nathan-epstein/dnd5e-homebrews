# Grok

**Race:** Half-Orc  
**Class:** Barbarian  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 6'3"  

## Personality
An intimidating but good-natured warrior. Grok speaks in short sentences and prefers action to words. Beneath the angry exterior is a loyal and earnest companion.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 14 | +2 |
| DEX | 10 | +0 |
| WIS | 13 | +1 |
| INT | 8 | -1 |
| CHA | 12 | +1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 12
- **Armor Class:** 10 (unarmored; can use leather for AC 11)
- **Initiative:** +0

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** STR +4, CON +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +1  
**Proficiencies:** Simple and martial melee weapons, shields

## Equipment

- Greataxe (or two Handaxes)
- Leather armor
- Explorer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Rage:** Bonus action to enter rage (2 rages per rest)
- While raging: +2 damage on melee STR attacks, resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing damage
- Lasts 1 minute or until you stop as a bonus action
- Cannot cast spells while raging

**Reckless Attack:** When attacking on your turn, you can attack recklessly (gain advantage on attack rolls but enemies gain advantage on attacks against you until your next turn)

## Notes

Grok is simple and powerful. Rage for damage resistance, attack with advantage (recklessly) for big hits. Perfect for beginners who like hitting things hard.
