# Kai

**Race:** Human  
**Class:** Monk  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'7"  

## Personality
A disciplined and focused warrior-mystic. Kai moves with grace and speaks thoughtfully. There's a quiet intensity about them, as though always centered and aware.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| DEX | 15 | +2 |
| WIS | 14 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| STR | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 9
- **Armor Class:** 15 (unarmored defense)
- **Initiative:** +2

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** STR +3, DEX +4  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +4, Insight +4, Stealth +4  
**Proficiencies:** Simple melee weapons, shortswords

## Equipment

- Quarterstaff (or Dagger)
- Explorer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel
- Prayer beads or meditation focus

## Class Features

**Martial Arts:** 
- Unarmed strikes count as melee weapon attacks and use DEX instead of STR
- Unarmed damage: 1d4 (or 1d6 with quarterstaff)
- Can attack twice per turn (attack and bonus unarmed strike)

**Ki Points:** 1 ki point (regain all on rest)
- Spend 1 ki to take Dodge as bonus action
- Spend 1 ki to take Disengage as bonus action

**Unarmored Defense:** AC = 10 + DEX + WIS (already calculated: 15)

## Cantrips

- **Prestidigitation:** Minor magical trick (mute sounds, light a candle, warm food, etc.)

## Notes

Kai is mobile and defensive. Use Martial Arts to attack twice, spend ki to dodge or escape. Good for players who like tactical positioning and acrobatic flair.
