# Elowen

**Race:** Wood Elf  
**Class:** Ranger  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'5"  

## Personality
A sharp-eyed scout with a deep connection to nature. Elowen is observant and careful, always scanning for threats. They speak to animals as easily as people and prefer the wilds to civilization.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| DEX | 15 | +2 |
| WIS | 14 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| STR | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 10
- **Armor Class:** 15 (leather armor, DEX)
- **Initiative:** +2

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** STR +3, DEX +4  
**Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +4, Survival +4, Nature +2  
**Proficiencies:** Simple and martial weapons

## Equipment

- Longbow (or Shortsword)
- Shortsword (or Dagger)
- Leather armor
- Explorer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel
- Animal handling tools

## Class Features

**Favored Enemy:** Beasts (advantage on attack rolls and WIS checks to track beasts)

**Natural Explorer:** Grasslands or forests (add +2 to navigation and survival checks in these terrains)

**Ranger Spellcasting:** Knows 2 spells (cast using WIS)
- **Cantrips (at-will):** Prestidigitation
- **1st-level spells (1 per rest):** Hunter's Mark (bonus action, deal +1d6 damage to marked target once per turn), Goodberry (bonus action, create berries that heal allies)

## Notes

Elowen is a ranged striker with good utility. Attack with bow, use Hunter's Mark for extra damage, heal with Goodberry if needed. Good tracking and perception skills.
