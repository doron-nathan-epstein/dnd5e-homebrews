# Misha

**Race:** Halfling  
**Class:** Rogue  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'0"  

## Personality
A quick-witted, nimble rogue with a sharp tongue and sharper reflexes. Misha laughs easily and takes risks others wouldn't. Underneath the charm is a calculating mind always looking for angles and advantages.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| DEX | 15 | +2 |
| INT | 14 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| STR | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 8
- **Armor Class:** 15 (leather armor, DEX)
- **Initiative:** +2

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** DEX +4, INT +4  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +4, Sleight of Hand +4, Stealth +6, Investigation +4, Perception +3  
**Proficiencies:** Simple melee weapons, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords

## Equipment

- Shortsword (or Rapier)
- Dagger
- Leather armor
- Shortbow with arrows (20)
- Explorer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel
- Thieves' tools

## Class Features

**Expertise:** Double proficiency bonus on Stealth and Sleight of Hand (+4 to both)

**Sneak Attack:** Once per turn, when you hit with a weapon attack and have advantage on the roll (or an ally is nearby), deal +1d6 damage

**Cunning Action:** Bonus action each turn to Dash, Disengage, or Hide

## Notes

Misha is mobile and damaging. Use Cunning Action to move safely, position for Sneak Attack advantage, and strike hard. Great for new players learning tactical positioning. Can investigate and pick locks with thieves' tools.
