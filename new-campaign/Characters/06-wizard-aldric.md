# Aldric

**Race:** High Elf  
**Class:** Wizard  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'9"  

## Personality
A scholarly mage with an insatiable curiosity. Aldric speaks carefully and observes constantly, always seeking knowledge. He's somewhat awkward in social situations but brilliant when discussing magic or mysteries.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| INT | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 14 | +2 |
| DEX | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| STR | 8 | -1 |
| CHA | 10 | +0 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 6
- **Armor Class:** 13 (no armor, DEX bonus)
- **Initiative:** +1

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** INT +4, WIS +3  
**Skills:** Arcana +4, Investigation +4, Insight +3  
**Proficiencies:** Dagger, quarterstaff, light crossbow

## Equipment

- Dagger
- Spellbook (your spells are recorded here)
- Component pouch
- Scholar's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel
- Ink, quill, and parchment

## Class Features

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using INT (spell save DC 12)

**Cantrips (at-will):** 
- **Fire Bolt:** Ranged attack 120 ft, 1d10 fire damage
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (2 prepared, 2 per rest):**
- **Mage Armor:** Bonus action, AC becomes 13 + DEX for 8 hours
- **Magic Missile:** Ranged, 3 magical darts that each hit for 1d4 + 1 damage
- **Detect Magic:** Ritual (1 hour), sense magical auras nearby
- **Sleep:** Area effect, put up to 5d8 HP of creatures to sleep (great for many weak enemies)

## Notes

Aldric is a classic wizard—use Magic Missile or Fire Bolt in combat, prepare Mage Armor before fights to boost AC. Sleep is powerful against groups. Beginner-friendly caster with clear options.
