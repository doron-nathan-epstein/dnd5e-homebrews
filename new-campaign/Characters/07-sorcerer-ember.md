# Ember

**Race:** Tiefling  
**Class:** Sorcerer  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'8"  

## Personality
A fiery and impulsive mage whose magical power flows from within rather than study. Ember is passionate, emotional, and acts on instinct. Their infernal heritage marks them, but they're determined to forge their own path.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| CHA | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 14 | +2 |
| DEX | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 8 | -1 |
| STR | 10 | +0 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 7
- **Armor Class:** 13 (no armor, DEX bonus)
- **Initiative:** +1

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** CHA +4, CON +4  
**Skills:** Perception +3, Arcana +1, Deception +4  
**Proficiencies:** Dagger, quarterstaff

## Equipment

- Dagger
- Component pouch
- Scholar's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using CHA (spell save DC 12, spell attack bonus +4)

**Sorcery Points:** 1 point per rest (spend to modify spell effects)
- Spend to add 1d4 damage to a spell
- Spend to reroll damage dice on a spell

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Fire Bolt:** Ranged attack 120 ft, 1d10 fire damage
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (2 known, 2 per rest):**
- **Burning Hands:** 15-foot cone, creatures save or take 3d6 fire damage (great for groups)
- **Magic Missile:** Ranged, 3 magical darts that each hit for 1d4 + 1 damage
- **Chromatic Orb:** Single target, 1d8 damage of your choice type (excellent for focused damage)

## Notes

Ember is intuitive and powerful. Use Sorcery Points to boost damage, cast Burning Hands against groups, or focus Fire Bolt on single targets. Charisma-based magic feels different from Wizard—spontaneous and personal.
