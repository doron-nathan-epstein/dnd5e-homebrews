# Vex

**Race:** Human  
**Class:** Warlock  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'10"  

## Personality
A mysterious figure marked by a pact with something otherworldly. Vex is calculating and intense, with an unsettling confidence about what they can do. They speak in riddles sometimes and seem to know things they shouldn't.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| CHA | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 14 | +2 |
| DEX | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| STR | 8 | -1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 7
- **Armor Class:** 13 (no armor, DEX bonus)
- **Initiative:** +1

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** CHA +4, WIS +3  
**Skills:** Arcana +2, Deception +4, Insight +3, Perception +3  
**Proficiencies:** Simple weapons, dagger

## Equipment

- Dagger
- Component pouch
- Scholar's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using CHA (spell save DC 12, spell attack bonus +4)

**Pact Boon:** Pact of the Archfey (gain a small magical creature as a familiar)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Eldritch Blast:** Ranged attack 120 ft, 1d10 force damage (this is your primary attack)
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**Warlock Spells (1st-level, 1 known, 1 per rest—but cast at higher power):**
- **Charm Person:** Target WIS save, become friendly to you for 1 hour (excellent for control)
- **Hex:** Bonus action, curse a target for 1 hour—they have disadvantage on one ability check you choose, and take 1d6 damage when you hit them (stacks with damage)

## Notes

Vex is unique—they don't have spell slots like other casters. Instead, spells recharge on a short rest (even just 10 minutes). Eldritch Blast is the backbone attack. Hex adds flavor and damage. Works differently from other casters, which feels mysterious and pact-powered.
