# Sister Grace

**Race:** Dwarf  
**Class:** Cleric  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 4'10"  

## Personality
A devoted healer with an unshakeable faith. Grace speaks with quiet authority and radiates calm even in chaos. She's compassionate but firm, and will not hesitate to defend her allies with spell and mace.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| WIS | 15 | +2 |
| STR | 14 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| CHA | 12 | +1 |
| DEX | 10 | +0 |
| INT | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 9
- **Armor Class:** 16 (chain mail)
- **Initiative:** +0

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** WIS +4, CHA +3  
**Skills:** Medicine +4, Insight +4, Perception +2  
**Proficiencies:** Simple weapons, all armor and shields

## Equipment

- Mace
- Shield
- Chain mail
- Holy symbol (on breastplate)
- Priest's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using WIS (spell save DC 12, spell attack bonus +4)

**Divine Domain:** Life Domain (bonus healing)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Guidance:** Touch target, they gain +1d4 on next ability check (for puzzles!)
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (2 prepared, 2 per rest):**
- **Healing Word:** Ranged 60 ft, heal an ally for 1d4 + WIS
- **Cure Wounds:** Touch target, heal for 1d8 + WIS
- **Bless:** Bonus action, up to 3 allies gain +1d4 to attack rolls and saves for 1 minute
- **Guiding Bolt:** Ranged 120 ft, 1d10 radiant damage, next attack against target has advantage

## Notes

Grace is a healing powerhouse and solid defender. Healing Word keeps allies alive at range, Bless boosts the party, Guiding Bolt deals damage and helps allies. Great for new players learning support and healing.
