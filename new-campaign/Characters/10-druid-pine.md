# Pine

**Race:** Wood Elf  
**Class:** Druid  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'6"  

## Personality
A quiet naturalist who views the world as a connected web of life. Pine moves with the ease of someone at home in wild places. They're thoughtful and sometimes seem to listen more to the forest than to people.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| WIS | 15 | +2 |
| CON | 14 | +2 |
| DEX | 13 | +1 |
| STR | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 10
- **Armor Class:** 13 (leather armor, DEX, DEX bonus from Unarmored Defense if in wild shape)
- **Initiative:** +1

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** WIS +4, INT +2  
**Skills:** Medicine +4, Nature +2, Perception +4, Survival +4  
**Proficiencies:** Simple melee weapons

## Equipment

- Quarterstaff (or Dagger)
- Leather armor
- Component pouch
- Dungeoneer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel
- Druidic focus (staff or totem)

## Class Features

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using WIS (spell save DC 12, spell attack bonus +4)

**Wild Shape:** Can transform into a beast (limited to small beasts at Level 1, like wolf or hawk)
- Gain the beast's AC and HP, keep your mental stats
- Can stay transformed for 10 minutes per rest
- Revert if reduced to 0 HP

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Thorn Whip:** Ranged 30 ft melee attack, 1d6 piercing damage and pull target 5 ft closer
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (2 prepared, 2 per rest):**
- **Entangle:** Area 20 ft, creatures save or are restrained by magical vines
- **Goodberry:** Bonus action, create berries that heal allies (1d4 + WIS per berry)
- **Healing Word:** Ranged 60 ft, heal an ally for 1d4 + WIS
- **Thunderwave:** 15-foot radius, creatures save or take 1d8 thunder damage and are pushed 5 ft

## Notes

Pine is versatile—heal with Goodberry, control crowds with Entangle or Thunderwave, or transform into a beast for melee combat. Herbalism knowledge and nature connection provide good flavor. Good for players who like options.
