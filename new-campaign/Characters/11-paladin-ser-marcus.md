# Ser Marcus

**Race:** Human  
**Class:** Paladin  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'11"  

## Personality
A righteous warrior bound by oath and conviction. Marcus speaks with unwavering certainty and leads by example. There's a quiet intensity about him—a sense that he's seen darkness and chosen light anyway.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 15 | +2 |
| CHA | 14 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| DEX | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 10
- **Armor Class:** 18 (plate armor)
- **Initiative:** -1

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** STR +4, CHA +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Insight +3, Medicine +3  
**Proficiencies:** All simple and martial weapons, all armor and shields

## Equipment

- Longsword (or Greatsword)
- Shield
- Plate armor
- Holy symbol
- Priest's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Divine Sense:** Sense celestials, fiends, and undead within 60 ft (great for detecting threats)

**Lay on Hands:** Pool of 5 HP per rest to heal allies or deal damage to undead
- Touch action, spend any amount to heal an ally

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using CHA (spell save DC 12, spell attack bonus +4)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Sacred Flame:** Ranged 60 ft, creature save or takes 1d8 radiant damage (doesn't require melee attacks)
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (2 prepared, 2 per rest):**
- **Bless:** Bonus action, up to 3 allies gain +1d4 to attack rolls and saves for 1 minute
- **Command:** Ranged 60 ft, target CHA save or follow a one-word command (Flee, Drop, Grovel)
- **Cure Wounds:** Touch, heal 1d8 + CHA
- **Detect Good and Evil:** Ritual, sense alignment auras nearby

## Notes

Marcus is a hybrid healer/defender. High AC keeps him alive, Lay on Hands heals, Bless boosts the party, and Command controls enemies. Sacred Flame is reliable damage. Perfect for players who want to protect allies while dealing damage.
