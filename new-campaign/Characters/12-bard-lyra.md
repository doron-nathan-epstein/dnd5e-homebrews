# Lyra

**Race:** Half-Elf  
**Class:** Bard  
**Level:** 1  
**Height:** 5'7"  

## Personality
A charming storyteller and musician who draws people in with wit and warmth. Lyra reads people like books and knows exactly what to say. There's a hint of mystery beneath the charm—layers most don't see.

## Ability Scores

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| CHA | 15 | +2 |
| DEX | 14 | +2 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| WIS | 12 | +1 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| STR | 8 | -1 |

## Combat Stats

- **Hit Points:** 8
- **Armor Class:** 15 (leather armor, DEX)
- **Initiative:** +2

## Skills & Proficiencies

**Saving Throws:** CHA +4, DEX +4  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +4, Deception +4, Insight +3, Perception +3, Performance +4, Persuasion +4, Stealth +4  
**Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords

## Equipment

- Rapier (or Shortsword)
- Dagger
- Lute (or other instrument)
- Leather armor
- Entertainer's pack
- Waterskins (4)
- Bedroll
- 50 feet of hemp rope
- Flint & steel

## Class Features

**Spellcasting:** Cast spells using CHA (spell save DC 12, spell attack bonus +4)

**Bardic Inspiration:** Use a bonus action to inspire an ally within 60 ft
- They gain 1d6 inspiration die (can be added to one ability check, attack roll, or save within 1 minute)
- Can inspire 1 creature per rest at this level

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Vicious Mockery:** Ranged 60 ft, creature WIS save or takes 1d4 psychic damage and has disadvantage on next attack
- **Light:** Illuminate an object or area for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (2 known, 2 per rest):**
- **Cure Wounds:** Touch, heal 1d8 + CHA
- **Healing Word:** Ranged 60 ft, heal an ally for 1d4 + CHA
- **Disguise Self:** Illusion, change appearance for 1 hour
- **Tasha's Hideous Laughter:** Ranged 30 ft, creature WIS save or is incapacitated by laughter for 1 minute

## Notes

Lyra is a skill expert and support caster. Bardic Inspiration boosts allies' checks (great for puzzles!), healing spells keep allies alive, and Vicious Mockery deals damage while debuffing. High skill bonuses make Lyra great for investigation and social encounters. Versatile and fun for experienced players who like having options.
