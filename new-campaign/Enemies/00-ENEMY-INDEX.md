## Quick Reference: Enemy Progression

**Puzzle 2 (Level 1):**
- 12 reflections, each Level 1
- Each is a weaker copy of the corresponding PC
- Total CR: ~5 (very beatable for a Level 1 party)

**Puzzle 4 (Level 2):**
- 1 Sand Guardian (CR 2)
- 1 Scarab Swarm (CR 1)
- Total CR: ~3 (medium challenge for a Level 2 party)

**Puzzle 5 (Level 3):**
- 1 Kaelthys (CR 12)
- BUT the riddle mechanic makes this more tactical than raw combat
- Total CR: ~12 (hard, but beatable with clever riddle-solving)
