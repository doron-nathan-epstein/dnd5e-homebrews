# Grok's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Grok the Barbarian**. This reflection fights with primal fury—simple but devastating.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Grok's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (half-orc)  
**AC:** 10 (unarmored)  
**HP:** 12 (1d12 + 2 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 | 10 | 14 | 8 | 13 | 12 |
| +2 | +0 | +2 | -1 | +1 | +1 |

**Saving Throws:** STR +4, CON +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +1  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Actions

**Greataxe:** *Melee weapon attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d12 + 2) slashing damage.

**Rage (1/rest, Bonus Action):** Enter rage for up to 1 minute or until it ends.
- While raging: add +2 to damage on STR melee attacks
- Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing damage
- Cannot cast spells while raging

---

## Combat Behavior

- Uses Rage on Turn 1 or 2
- Attacks once per turn with Greataxe
- Tries to flank the player
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Makes basic tactical decisions (attack the closest enemy)
- Fights silently (no taunts)

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is tough and direct—high HP, straightforward attacks, Rage for damage boost. Fighting a stronger but less tactical version of yourself.
