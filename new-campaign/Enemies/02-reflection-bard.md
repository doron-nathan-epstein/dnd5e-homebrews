# Lyra's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Lyra the Bard**. This reflection uses inspiration, healing, and crowd control through spells and abilities.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Lyra's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (half-elf)  
**AC:** 13 (leather armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 9 (1d8 + 1 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 | 13 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 15 |
| +0 | +1 | +1 | +1 | +1 | +2 |

**Saving Throws:** DEX +3, CHA +4  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +3, Performance +4, Persuasion +4, Deception +4, Insight +3  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common, Elvish  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4 (CHA-based)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Vicious Mockery:** +4 to hit (CHA), 1d4 psychic damage, target has disadvantage on next attack (60 ft range)
- **Prestidigitation:** Minor spell effects

**1st-level Spells (1 per rest):**
- **Healing Word:** Ranged 60 ft, bonus action, heal target for 1d4 + CHA (2 HP)
- **Tasha's Hideous Laughter:** Ranged 60 ft, WIS save DC 12 or target is incapacitated by laughter for 1 minute
- **Thunderwave:** 15-foot radius, DEX save DC 12 or 1d8 thunder damage and push 5 ft

---

## Actions

**Rapier:** *Melee weapon attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

**Vicious Mockery (Cantrip):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 2) psychic damage, and the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

**Healing Word (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** Ranged 60 feet, heal self or an ally for 1d4 + 2 HP.

**Tasha's Hideous Laughter (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** Ranged 60 feet. Target makes a WIS save (DC 12) or falls prone and is incapacitated by laughter for 1 minute (or until it takes damage).

**Thunderwave (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** 15-foot radius centered on the reflection. Each creature in the area makes a DEX save (DC 12) or takes 4 (1d8) thunder damage and is pushed 5 ft away.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Uses Vicious Mockery from range to apply disadvantage and damage
- Casts Tasha's Hideous Laughter to incapacitate the player (devastating if successful)
- Uses Healing Word to heal itself if reduced below 5 HP
- Attacks with Rapier if forced into melee
- Tries to stay 40+ feet away if possible
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is a support/control caster. Vicious Mockery is ranged spam with debuff, Tasha's Hideous Laughter is deadly control (incapacitation), and Healing Word keeps it alive. Medium durability (9 HP, AC 13). The main threat is incapacitation—if the player falls prone and laughing, the reflection is free to pummel them.
