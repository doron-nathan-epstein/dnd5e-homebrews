# Sister Grace's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Sister Grace the Cleric**. This reflection is armored, healing, and deals radiant damage.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Sister Grace's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (dwarf)  
**AC:** 16 (chain mail)  
**HP:** 9 (1d8 + 1 CON)  
**Speed:** 25 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14 | 10 | 13 | 8 | 15 | 12 |
| +2 | +0 | +1 | -1 | +2 | +1 |

**Saving Throws:** WIS +4, CHA +3  
**Skills:** Medicine +4, Insight +4, Perception +2  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 12  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Sacred Flame:** +4 to hit (WIS), 1d8 radiant damage (60 ft range, doesn't require melee attacks)
- **Light:** Illuminate an object for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (1 per rest):**
- **Healing Word:** Ranged 60 ft, heal an ally for 1d4 + WIS
- **Cure Wounds:** Touch target, heal for 1d8 + WIS
- **Bless:** Bonus action, up to 3 allies gain +1d4 to attack rolls and saves for 1 minute
- **Guiding Bolt:** Ranged 120 ft, 1d10 radiant damage, next attack against target has advantage

---

## Actions

**Mace:** *Melee weapon attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

**Sacred Flame (Cantrip):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 2) radiant damage.

**Healing Word (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** Ranged 60 feet, heal self or an ally for 1d4 + 2 HP.

**Guiding Bolt (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** Ranged 120 feet, +4 to hit, 1d10 radiant damage, and the next attack against that target has advantage.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Attacks with Sacred Flame from range
- Uses Mace if forced into melee (good AC for a reflection)
- Heals self if reduced below 5 HP
- Casts Guiding Bolt for damage + ally debuff
- Tries to stay 40+ feet away if possible
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is durable (9 HP, AC 16) and can heal itself. It doesn't deal huge damage, but it's hard to kill due to armor and self-healing. Prioritize damage to overcome the healing.
