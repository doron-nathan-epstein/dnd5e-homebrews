# Pine's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Pine the Druid**. This reflection uses control spells and nature magic.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Pine's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (wood elf)  
**AC:** 13 (leather armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 10 (1d8 + 2 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 | 13 | 14 | 10 | 15 | 8 |
| +1 | +1 | +2 | +0 | +2 | -1 |

**Saving Throws:** WIS +4, INT +2  
**Skills:** Medicine +4, Nature +2, Perception +4, Survival +4  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 14  
**Languages:** Common, Druidic  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4 (WIS-based)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Thorn Whip:** +4 to hit, 1d6 piercing damage, pull target 5 ft closer (30 ft range)
- **Light:** Illuminate an object for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (1 per rest):**
- **Entangle:** 20-foot radius within 90 feet, DEX save DC 12 or restrained by vines
- **Goodberry:** Bonus action, create berries that heal (1d4 + WIS per berry)
- **Healing Word:** Ranged 60 ft, heal an ally for 1d4 + WIS
- **Thunderwave:** 15-foot radius within 5 feet, DEX save DC 12 or 1d8 thunder damage and push 5 ft

---

## Actions

**Quarterstaff:** *Melee weapon attack:* +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

**Thorn Whip (Cantrip):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, and the target is pulled 5 ft closer.

**Entangle (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** 20-foot radius within 90 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DEX save (DC 12) or be restrained by magical vines for 1 minute.

**Goodberry (1st-level spell, bonus action, 1/rest):** Create 1d4 + WIS berries (4 berries). The reflection or any creature can use an action to eat a berry and regain 1d4 + 2 HP.

**Thunderwave (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** 15-foot radius centered on the reflection. Each creature in the area makes a DEX save (DC 12) or takes 4 (1d8) thunder damage and is pushed 5 ft away.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Uses Thorn Whip to attack from range and control positioning
- Casts Entangle to restrain the player (devastating if successful)
- Uses Goodberry to heal itself
- Attacks with Quarterstaff if forced into melee
- Tries to stay 30+ feet away
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection uses control (Entangle) and healing (Goodberry). Medium durability (10 HP). Entangle is dangerous—if successful, the player is restrained and helpless. Pressure this reflection before it can cast control spells.
