# Thorne's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Thorne the Fighter**. This reflection fights exactly like a new fighter would—simple tactics, reliable attacks, straightforward strategy.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Thorne's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (human)  
**AC:** 16 (chain mail)  
**HP:** 11 (1d10 + 2 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 | 13 | 14 | 10 | 12 | 8 |
| +2 | +1 | +2 | +0 | +1 | -1 |

**Saving Throws:** STR +4, CON +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +1  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Actions

**Longsword:** *Melee weapon attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage. Or 7 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Fighting Style (Duelist):** When wielding a melee weapon and no shield, add +2 to damage rolls. This is already included in the attack above.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Attacks once per turn with Longsword
- Flanks the player if possible
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Makes tactically sound but simple decisions
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve into golden dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection mirrors the player's fighting style at Level 1. It's straightforward—honest combat with no tricks. The unsettling part is fighting a version of yourself.
