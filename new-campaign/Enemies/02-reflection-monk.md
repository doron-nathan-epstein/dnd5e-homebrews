# Kai's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Kai the Monk**. This reflection is mobile, defensive, and attacks twice per turn using martial arts.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Kai's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (human)  
**AC:** 15 (Unarmored Defense: 10 + 2 DEX + 2 WIS)  
**HP:** 9 (1d8 + 1 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 | 15 | 13 | 10 | 14 | 8 |
| +1 | +2 | +1 | +0 | +2 | -1 |

**Saving Throws:** STR +3, DEX +4  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +4, Insight +4, Stealth +4  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 12  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

**Martial Arts:** Can use unarmed strikes and use DEX instead of STR for melee attacks. Unarmed damage is 1d4 + DEX.

---

## Actions

**Multiattack:** The reflection makes two unarmed strikes.

**Unarmed Strike:** *Melee weapon attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

**Flurry of Blows (1 Ki, Bonus Action):** Make two unarmed strikes as a bonus action.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Attacks twice per turn using Martial Arts
- Uses Flurry of Blows early for extra damage
- Mobile—tries to move around the player, avoiding being trapped
- Flanks if possible
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve into golden dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is fast and nimble. Medium damage but high AC. Uses Ki to attack multiple times per turn. Emphasizes mobility and defensive positioning.
