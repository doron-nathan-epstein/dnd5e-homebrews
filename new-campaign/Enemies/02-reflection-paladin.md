# Ser Garrett's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Ser Garrett the Paladin**. This reflection is heavily armored, deals divine damage, and uses holy smite.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Ser Garrett's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (human)  
**AC:** 18 (plate armor)  
**HP:** 11 (1d10 + 1 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 | 10 | 13 | 8 | 12 | 14 |
| +3 | +0 | +1 | -1 | +1 | +2 |

**Saving Throws:** WIS +3, CHA +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +5, Insight +3, Perception +3  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4 (CHA-based)

**1st-level Spells (1 per rest):**
- **Divine Favor:** Bonus action, deal extra 1d4 radiant damage on weapon attacks for 1 minute
- **Thunderous Smite:** Bonus action, next weapon attack deals extra 1d6 thunder damage and push target 5 ft

---

## Actions

**Longsword:** *Melee weapon attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.

**Divine Favor (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** For 1 minute, the reflection's weapon attacks deal an extra 1d4 radiant damage.

**Thunderous Smite (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** The reflection's next weapon attack deals an extra 1d6 thunder damage, and the target must make a STR save (DC 12) or be pushed 5 ft away.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Activates Divine Favor or Thunderous Smite on Turn 1
- Advances and attacks with Longsword
- Uses heavy armor to tank hits (AC 18, highest of all reflections)
- Fights in melee range; doesn't retreat
- Never backs away; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is a tank. Highest AC (18) and good HP (11). Deals solid damage with Longsword + Divine Favor or Thunderous Smite. Hard to kill due to armor but relatively straightforward—advance and hit hard.
