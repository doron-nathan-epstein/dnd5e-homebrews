# Elowen's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Elowen the Ranger**. This reflection uses ranged attacks and Hunter's Mark for extra damage.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Elowen's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (wood elf)  
**AC:** 15 (leather armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 10 (1d10 + 1 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 | 15 | 13 | 10 | 14 | 8 |
| +1 | +2 | +1 | +0 | +2 | -1 |

**Saving Throws:** STR +3, DEX +4  
**Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +4, Survival +4, Nature +2  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 14  
**Languages:** Common, Elvish  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Actions

**Longbow:** *Ranged weapon attack:* +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.

**Hunter's Mark (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** Mark a target within 90 feet. For 1 hour, the next time this reflection hits the marked target each turn, it deals an extra 1d6 damage. Requires concentration.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Uses Hunter's Mark on Turn 1 or 2
- Attacks once per turn with Longbow from range
- Tries to stay 60+ feet away from the player
- Moves carefully to maintain distance
- Never retreats; fights until shattered (but won't move into melee if avoidable)
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is a ranged striker. Medium damage but uses Hunter's Mark for a damage boost (+1d6). Stays at distance and pelts the player with arrows.
