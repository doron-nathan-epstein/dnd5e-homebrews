# Misha's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Misha the Rogue**. This reflection is evasive, deals extra damage through Sneak Attack, and uses mobility to stay alive.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Misha's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (halfling)  
**AC:** 15 (leather armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 8 (1d8 + 0 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 | 15 | 13 | 14 | 12 | 8 |
| +0 | +2 | +1 | +2 | +1 | -1 |

**Saving Throws:** DEX +4, INT +4  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +4, Sleight of Hand +4, Stealth +6, Investigation +4, Perception +3  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Actions

**Shortsword:** *Melee weapon attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Sneak Attack (1/turn):** When the reflection's attack roll has advantage or a friendly creature is nearby, it deals an extra 1d6 damage (total: 1d6 + 2 + 1d6).

**Cunning Action (Bonus Action):** Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Tries to flank the player for Sneak Attack advantage
- Uses Cunning Action to Disengage and move safely
- Mobile and evasive—stays 20–30 feet away when possible
- Never retreats; fights until shattered (but won't get trapped)
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve into golden dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is tricky and evasive. Low HP but good AC and mobility. Uses Sneak Attack for extra damage and Cunning Action to stay safe. The hardest reflection to pin down.
