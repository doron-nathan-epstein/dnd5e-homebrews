# Lysa's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Lysa the Sorcerer**. This reflection is fragile but uses chaotic elemental magic.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Lysa's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (tiefling)  
**AC:** 12 (no armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 7 (1d6 + 2 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 | 12 | 14 | 10 | 11 | 15 |
| -1 | +1 | +2 | +0 | +0 | +2 |

**Saving Throws:** CHA +4, CON +4  
**Skills:** Arcana +2, Deception +4, Insight +2  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 10  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4 (CHA-based)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Fire Bolt:** +4 to hit, 1d10 fire damage (120 ft range)
- **Prestidigitation:** Minor spell effect

**1st-level Spells (2 per rest, sorcerer flexibility):**
- **Burning Hands:** 15-foot cone, DEX save DC 12 or 2d6 fire damage
- **Chromatic Orb:** Ranged 90 ft, +4 to hit, 3d8 damage (acid/cold/fire/lightning/poison/thunder based on sorcerer's choice)
- **Mage Armor:** Bonus action, AC becomes 13 + DEX for 8 hours (already calculated in AC)

---

## Actions

**Fire Bolt (Cantrip):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 2) fire damage.

**Burning Hands (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** 15-foot cone centered on the reflection. Each creature in the area makes a DEX save (DC 12) or takes 7 (2d6) fire damage.

**Chromatic Orb (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (3d8) damage (type varies: acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder).

---

## Combat Behavior

- Stays at range (60+ feet) and uses Fire Bolt
- Casts Burning Hands if the player is close or there are multiple enemies
- Casts Chromatic Orb for heavy damage if safe to do so
- Never moves into melee range
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is very fragile (7 HP) but dangerous. Fire Bolt is decent spam, Burning Hands is good for area control, and Chromatic Orb is high damage. Stay at distance and pressure this reflection before it casts.
