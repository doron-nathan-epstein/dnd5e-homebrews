# Kael's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Kael the Warlock**. This reflection uses eldritch blast and hexing abilities.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Kael's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (human)  
**AC:** 12 (no armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 8 (1d8 + 0 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 | 12 | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 |
| +0 | +1 | +0 | +1 | +1 | +2 |

**Saving Throws:** WIS +3, CHA +4  
**Skills:** Arcana +3, Insight +3, Perception +3  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/3 (75 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4 (CHA-based)

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Eldritch Blast:** +4 to hit, 1d10 force damage (120 ft range), target must make STR save DC 12 or be pushed 5 ft
- **Mage Hand Legerdemain:** Create invisible mage hand for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (1 per rest, warlock limitation):**
- **Hex:** Bonus action, mark a target within 90 feet. For 1 hour, target has disadvantage on ability checks using a chosen ability (e.g., STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA)
- **Armor of Agathys:** Bonus action, gain 5 temporary HP and deal 5 cold damage to melee attackers

---

## Actions

**Eldritch Blast (Cantrip):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 2) force damage. The target must make a STR save (DC 12) or be pushed 5 ft away.

**Hex (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** Mark a target within 90 feet. For 1 hour, the target has disadvantage on ability checks using a chosen ability (e.g., STR saves, DEX checks, etc.).

**Armor of Agathys (1st-level spell, 1/rest, Bonus Action):** Gain 5 temporary HP. Until the armor is broken, any creature that hits the reflection with a melee attack takes 5 cold damage.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Uses Hex on Turn 1 to apply disadvantage (often on STR or DEX to hinder combat)
- Activates Armor of Agathys on Turn 1 if the player is nearby
- Stands at range (60+ feet) and uses Eldritch Blast
- Never moves into melee range
- Never retreats; fights until shattered
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is a debuffer/damage dealer. Hex applies disadvantage to key abilities, and Armor of Agathys punishes melee fighters. Medium fragility (8 HP) but deceptive durability with temp HP and debuffs. Pressure it before Hex can take effect.
