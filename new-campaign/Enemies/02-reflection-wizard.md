# Aldric's Reflection

**Puzzle:** 2 (Hall of Shattered Selves)

---

## Overview

A weakened Level 1 version of **Aldric the Wizard**. This reflection is fragile but uses spells for ranged damage and control.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Aldric's Reflection  
**Size/Type:** Medium humanoid (high elf)  
**AC:** 13 (no armor, DEX)  
**HP:** 6 (1d6 + 2 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 | 13 | 14 | 15 | 12 | 10 |
| -1 | +1 | +2 | +2 | +1 | +0 |

**Saving Throws:** INT +4, WIS +3  
**Skills:** Arcana +4, Investigation +4, Insight +3  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

---

## Traits

**Mirrored Movement:** The reflection moves exactly as the PC does, but always half a heartbeat late. Purely flavor.

**Fragile Form:** Made of golden light and bone. Golden cracks run through its body. When reduced to 0 HP, it shatters into golden shards.

---

## Spellcasting

**Spell Save DC:** 12  
**Spell Attack Bonus:** +4

**Cantrips (at-will):**
- **Fire Bolt:** +4 to hit, 1d10 fire damage (120 ft range)
- **Light:** Illuminate an object for 1 hour

**1st-level Spells (1 per rest):**
- **Sleep:** Area 15 ft radius, up to 5d8 HP of creatures fall asleep
- **Magic Missile:** 3 darts, 1d4 + 1 force damage each (always hits)

---

## Actions

**Fire Bolt (Cantrip):** *Ranged spell attack:* +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) fire damage.

**Sleep (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** 15-foot radius within 90 feet. Up to 5d8 HP of creatures fall asleep for 1 hour. Creatures starting their turn in darkness are at disadvantage to resist. This is devastating if multiple enemies.

**Magic Missile (1st-level spell, 1/rest):** 3 darts, each 1d4 + 1 force damage. Automatically hit (no attack roll needed).

---

## Combat Behavior

- Stays at range (60+ feet) and uses Fire Bolt
- Casts Sleep if multiple enemies present (devastating)
- Casts Magic Missile if safe to do so
- Never moves into melee range
- Never retreats; fights until shattered (but keeps distance)
- Fights silently

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the reflection:
1. Stands perfectly still
2. Golden cracks race across its body
3. Shatters into thousands of golden shards
4. Shards dissolve instantly into dust
5. A faint whisper of the player's own voice (Level 1 tone) echoes:
   - "Why?"
   - "Again?"
   - "Become."
   - Or similar existential phrase

---

## Notes

This reflection is very fragile (6 HP) but dangerous. Sleep is a powerful spell—if this reflection casts it, the player is in trouble. Keep distance and pressure this reflection quickly before it can cast high-impact spells.
