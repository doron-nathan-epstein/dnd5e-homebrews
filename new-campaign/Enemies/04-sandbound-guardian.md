# Sandbound Guardian

**Puzzle:** 4 (Desert of Lost Echoes)

---

## Overview

A weakened sand elemental bound to the Soulwell. This encounter is an endurance test: the elemental is unkillable through normal means and must be avoided or worn down through clever spell use (water damage). The party should survive and escape, not fight to the death.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Sandbound Guardian  
**Size/Type:** Large elemental  
**AC:** 14 (sandstone hide)  
**HP:** 22 (3d10 + 3 CON)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 | 12 | 15 | 4 | 10 | 6 |
| +3 | +1 | +2 | -3 | +0 | -2 |

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, psychic, nonmagical weapon damage  
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing (nonmagical)  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Water, cold  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 10  
**Languages:** Terran  
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

---

## Traits

**Elemental Body:** The guardian is made of sand and stone. It can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 foot wide without squeezing. It is immune to damage from nonmagical weapons.

**Petrification Aura:** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the guardian must make a CON save (DC 12) or become petrified for 1 minute. If the creature takes damage from water or cold, this effect ends.

**Water Vulnerability:** When the guardian takes water or cold damage, it is slowed (speed halved) until the end of its next turn. If it takes 6+ damage from water, it is stunned until the end of its next turn.

---

## Actions

**Sand Spray (Recharge 5-6):** *Melee weapon attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target must make a DEX save (DC 12) or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

**Burrow:** The guardian burrows into sand/stone, becoming invisible and moving up to 30 feet per turn. While burrowed, it cannot be targeted by spells or attacks that require sight.

---

## Reactions

**Sand Shield:** When the guardian takes damage, it can use its reaction to reduce the damage by 1d6 + 2. It can do this 3 times per day.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Uses Sand Spray to blind and damage
- Burrows frequently to avoid damage and reposition
- Uses Sand Shield to reduce incoming damage
- Targets the weakest-looking party member
- Never pursues fleeing creatures beyond the encounter area
- Becomes significantly weakened if hit by water-based spells (Tidal Wave, Create Water, etc.)

---

## Defeat Condition

The guardian cannot be killed through melee combat alone. It requires water/cold damage or clever tactics. If the party casts water-based spells or uses Create Water repeatedly, it can be worn down. If the party simply flees the encounter area, the guardian does not pursue (end encounter, players escape).

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the guardian:
1. Freezes in place
2. Large cracks form across its body
3. Collapses into a pile of sand
4. Sand slowly dissolves into golden dust
5. The air smells of ancient stone and salt

---

## Notes

This is not a kill fight. The party should realize that water and cold damage are its weakness and either use those spells repeatedly or find another way past. A smart party might suggest they simply flee, which is acceptable (they escape with their lives and regain the tether strength). The fight is designed to drain resources and test morale, not to be a tactical victory.
