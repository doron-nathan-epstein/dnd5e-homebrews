# Scarab Swarm

**Puzzle:** 4 (Desert of Lost Echoes)

---

## Overview

A swarm of enchanted scarabs that burrow and climb. Fast, relentless, and hard to hit. Uses climbing and burrowing to stay mobile and unpredictable.

---

## Stat Block

**Name:** Scarab Swarm  
**Size/Type:** Swarm of tiny insects  
**AC:** 12 (chitinous exoskeletons)  
**HP:** 16 (3d8 + 3 CON)  
**Speed:** 20 ft., climb 20 ft., burrow 20 ft.

**Ability Scores**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 6 | 14 | 12 | 1 | 8 | 3 |
| -2 | +2 | +1 | -5 | -1 | -4 |

**Damage Resistances:** Piercing, slashing (nonmagical)  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Bludgeoning  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained  
**Senses:** Darkvision 30 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 9  
**Languages:** None  
**Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

---

## Traits

**Swarm:** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa. The swarm can move through any opening at least 1 foot wide without squeezing.

**Burrowing Mobility:** The swarm can burrow through sand or loose soil, leaving behind small tunnels. While burrowed, it is invisible but can still attack.

**Climbing Walls:** The swarm climbs at full speed and can cling to ceilings without making climbing checks.

**Vulnerability to Bludgeoning:** The swarm takes double damage from bludgeoning attacks (area spells, hammer blows, etc.). A single well-placed Thunderwave or Fireball can hurt it severely.

---

## Actions

**Swarm Attack (Bite):** *Melee weapon attack:* +4 to hit, reach 0 ft., one target in the swarm's space. *Hit:* 5 (2d4 + 1) piercing damage. A creature can grapple the swarm as a bonus action if it makes a successful STR check (DC 12).

**Burrow (Bonus Action):** The swarm burrows into sand/soil and becomes invisible. It can move up to 20 feet while burrowed and can emerge as a bonus action.

---

## Combat Behavior

- Swarms the party, attempting to overwhelm with numbers
- Burrows and emerges from unexpected angles
- Climbs walls and ceilings to attack from above
- Focuses on one target until it falls
- Never retreats; fights to death
- Fights silently (only the sound of chittering mandibles)

---

## Defeating the Swarm

The swarm is vulnerable to:
- **Bludgeoning damage** (double damage): Thunderwave, Fireball, AOE spell attacks
- **Cold damage**: Slows the swarm (speed halved until next turn)
- **Fire damage**: Ordinary effectiveness but aesthetically thematic
- **Grappling:** A single creature can grapple the swarm with a successful STR check (DC 12)

The swarm is resistant to piercing and slashing (normal weapons bounce off chitin).

---

## Death

When reduced to 0 HP, the swarm:
1. Movements become erratic and sluggish
2. Scarabs begin to drop, unmoving
3. The air fills with the sound of clicking, then silence
4. Swarm dissolves into inanimate beetles that crumble to dust
5. A faint hum of magic fades

---

## Notes

This swarm is fast, mobile, and aggressive. It's not as durable as the Sand Elemental (16 HP vs 22) but can be dangerous if the party wastes time on piercing/slashing attacks. Smart players will use bludgeoning, AOE magic, or cold damage. The climbing and burrowing make it tactically interesting.
