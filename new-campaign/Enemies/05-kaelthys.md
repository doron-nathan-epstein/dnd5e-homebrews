# Kaelthys, The Sphinx of Sorrows

**Puzzle:** 5 (The Soulwell's Heart / Final Boss)

---

## Overview

**Kaelthys** is an ancient, weary sphinx corrupted by proximity to the Soulwell. Once a guardian, now a bitter prisoner who forces souls to answer riddles as the price of passage. At Level 2, the party faces a CR 12 encounter modified for their level. The fight combines combat and riddle-solving: answering riddles correctly damages the sphinx, while incorrect answers heal it and damage the party.

---

## Traits

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If Kaelthys fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Innate Spellcasting:** Kaelthys's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- **At-will:** detect magic, mage hand
- **3/day:** fireball, lightning bolt, dispel magic
- **1/day:** wish (limited, cannot be used in combat)

**Magic Resistance:** Kaelthys has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.

**The Riddle's Curse:** Once per round, Kaelthys poses a riddle to one creature it can see. The creature must make an INT save (DC 16) or lose its action on its next turn as it puzzles over the answer. This is flavor and mechanical—it doesn't prevent reactions or bonus actions, only the action itself.

---

## Actions

**Multiattack:** Kaelthys makes two claw attacks or two wing buffet attacks.

**Claw:** *Melee weapon attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

**Wing Buffet:** *Melee weapon attack:* +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a STR save (DC 15) or be knocked prone.

**Fireball (3/day):** 20-foot radius sphere within 150 feet. Each creature in the area makes a DEX save (DC 14) or takes 22 (4d10) fire damage.

**Lightning Bolt (3/day):** 100-foot line, 5 feet wide. Each creature in the line makes a DEX save (DC 14) or takes 22 (4d10) lightning damage.

---

## Legendary Actions

Kaelthys can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Kaelthys regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

**Claw Attack:** Kaelthys makes one claw attack.

**Wing Buffet:** Kaelthys makes one wing buffet attack.

**Fly:** Kaelthys flies up to its flying speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Cast a Spell (Costs 2 Actions):** Kaelthys casts a spell from its innate list.

**Pose a Riddle (Costs 1 Action):** Kaelthys poses a riddle to a creature within 60 feet (see The Riddle Mechanic below).

---

## The Riddle Mechanic

Once per round (as a bonus action), **Kaelthys poses a riddle to one creature it can see**. The creature must make an INT save (DC 16) to correctly answer the riddle:

### The Five Riddles (Elemental Progression)

**Riddle 1 (Air/Wind):**  
*"I am invisible, yet you feel my touch. I bend the trees but have no form. What am I?"*  
**Answer:** Wind / Air  
**DC 16 INT save**  
- **Success:** The creature deals 10 radiant damage to Kaelthys with its next attack, and the creature gains 2d4 temporary HP
- **Failure:** Kaelthys heals 15 HP, and the creature takes 4 psychic damage

**Riddle 2 (Water/River):**  
*"I flow but never walk, I can drown but have no lungs. Men fear me, yet I give life. What am I?"*  
**Answer:** Water / River / Ocean  
**DC 16 INT save**  
- **Success:** The creature deals 10 radiant damage to Kaelthys with its next attack, and the creature gains 2d4 temporary HP
- **Failure:** Kaelthys heals 15 HP, and the creature takes 4 psychic damage

**Riddle 3 (Earth):**  
*"I am solid and still, the foundation of all. Empires crumble upon me, yet I remain unchanged. What am I?"*  
**Answer:** Earth / Stone / Ground / Mountain  
**DC 16 INT save**  
- **Success:** The creature deals 10 radiant damage to Kaelthys with its next attack, and the creature gains 2d4 temporary HP
- **Failure:** Kaelthys heals 15 HP, and the creature takes 4 psychic damage

**Riddle 4 (Fire):**  
*"I consume but am not hunger. I warm but can also destroy. I can be born from air and water but live only briefly. What am I?"*  
**Answer:** Fire / Flame  
**DC 16 INT save**  
- **Success:** The creature deals 10 radiant damage to Kaelthys with its next attack, and the creature gains 2d4 temporary HP
- **Failure:** Kaelthys heals 15 HP, and the creature takes 4 psychic damage

**Riddle 5 (Spirit/True Name):**  
*"I am what you are, yet I am not you. I know your deepest truth, yet you refuse to see me. What am I?"*  
**Answer:** Soul / Spirit / Self / True Name / Heart  
**DC 16 INT save**  
- **Success:** The creature deals 10 radiant damage to Kaelthys with its next attack, and the creature gains 2d4 temporary HP
- **Failure:** Kaelthys heals 15 HP, and the creature takes 4 psychic damage

## Combat Behavior

- Opens with Fireball or Lightning Bolt to separate the party
- Uses Claw and Wing Buffet attacks in melee range
- Poses one riddle per round (favoring the strongest-looking party member)
- Flies around the arena (60 ft flying speed) to stay safe
- Uses Legendary Actions to attack after player turns
- Never retreats; fights to death
- Ignores enemies who are incapacitated by laughter (Tasha's Hideous Laughter) or other control effects

---

## Victory Conditions

### Clean Victory (3+ Riddles Answered Correctly)
- Kaelthys is banished in a flash of golden light
- The sphinx dissipates with a final, despairing cry
- The Soulwell stabilizes, and the party feels the tether strengthen permanently
- The sphinx's prison is broken; the soul echoes fade

### Pyrrhic Victory (<3 Riddles Answered Correctly)
- The sphinx is defeated but doesn't dissipate cleanly
- It clings to existence, cursing the party as it fades
- The Soulwell remains unstable; the tether is damaged
- **Consequence:** Each party member must make a WIS save (DC 16) or lose one memory (DM's choice: forget a spell, forget a key piece of information, suffer brief amnesia, etc.)
- The soul echoes linger longer than expected, and the party feels haunted

### Total Defeat
- If Kaelthys reduces the party to 0 HP before all riddles are solved, the party "wakes" at the beginning of Puzzle 5 with injuries (temporary -2 to all rolls until a short rest)
- This represents the Soulwell rejecting their souls

---

## Death

When Kaelthys is defeated with 3+ correct riddles:
1. The sphinx freezes mid-roar
2. Golden light erupts from its form
3. It dissolves into pure energy
4. The Soulwell pulses with power
5. The party feels the tether strengthen—they are no longer prisoners

When Kaelthys is defeated with <3 correct riddles:
1. The sphinx falls to one knee, still snarling
2. Its form destabilizes, cracks appearing in its stone body
3. It speaks one final curse in a language almost understood
4. It collapses and fades, but slowly—almost reluctantly
5. The Soulwell stabilizes but remains tainted; the party feels the tether weaken

## Modifications for Party Level

**At Level 2 (Recommended):**
- HP: 72 (as above)
- AC: 17
- Riddle DC: 16 (challenging but passable with +4 INT mod)
- Spell Damage: 22 (4d10) per spell
- Legendary Actions: All as above

**Harder (Add if the party is crushing the encounter):**
- Add another action per round: "Kaelthys can pose 2 riddles per round instead of 1"
- Increase riddle DC to 17
- Add a lair action: "At the start of Kaelthys's turn, it can create a 20-foot radius sphere of magical darkness centered on a point it can see within 120 feet. The sphere lasts until the end of its next turn"

**Easier (Use if the party is struggling):**
- Reduce HP to 54
- Reduce riddle DC to 15
- Remove one use of Legendary Resistance per day (allow 2 instead of 3)

---

## Notes

This is the climactic encounter. The riddles are the core mechanic—combat is just the wrapper. A smart party will realize that answering riddles correctly is the path to victory, not just hitting hard. The sphinx is tough but not invincible. Its AC (17) is high, but its spell saves are lower. Dispel Magic can shut down some of its effects. The riddle mechanic rewards problem-solving and roleplay over pure damage output.
