# The Soulwell Chamber: DM Master Guide

**A 3-hour D&D 5e One-Shot for 5 Level 1 Players**

---

## Overview

This guide is your complete toolkit for running *The Soulwell Chamber*. It contains campaign structure, rules clarifications, running advice, and contingencies.

**The Campaign in One Paragraph:**  
Five adventurers awaken in a mysterious stone chamber, tethered to a cosmic entity above called the Soulwell. They must solve five progressively difficult puzzles—solving riddles, fighting their own reflections, casting elemental magic, and enduring combat—while answering riddles posed by an ancient sphinx. Success means freedom and their minds intact; failure means the Soulwell claims their souls (with memory consequences). The campaign unfolds over exactly 3 hours of real-time play.

## Campaign Overview

### The Soulwell

An ancient cosmic entity or construct that feeds on souls and minds. It tethers the PCs magically, keeping them trapped in its chamber. Kaelthys, the ancient sphinx, is its guardian—corrupted by millennia of service.

**What PCs know:**
- They're in a strange chamber
- They're tethered to something above (the Soulwell)
- They must survive puzzles and combat to escape

**What PCs don't know (until the end):**
- Their souls are literally feeding the Soulwell as they solve puzzles
- Each puzzle completion makes the tether stronger (but also weakens Kaelthys)
- Solving riddles during the final boss fight empowers the sigils to break the tether

### Tone

**Atmosphere:** Eerie, mysterious, with moments of wonder and dread. The chamber feels *alive* and *hungry*.

**Pacing:** Fast-moving. No downtime. Each puzzle leads directly to the next.

**Threat Level:** Medium. Puzzles are solvable; combat is challenging but survivable for a Level 1–3 party that works together.

### Victory Conditions

**Clean Victory (3+ riddles answered):** Kaelthys is banished. The Soulwell collapses. PCs escape with memories intact (or fading naturally).

**Pyrrhic Victory (<3 riddles answered):** PCs survive, but Kaelthys dies partially. The Soulwell's grip lingers. One PC suffers a permanent or semi-permanent consequence (curse, lost HP, exhaustion).

**Defeat:** If Kaelthys kills all PCs before any riddles are solved, Kaelthys wins. The PCs are absorbed by the Soulwell. (This is unlikely.)

## Character Selection & Setup

### How to Run Character Selection

**Time Allocation:** 10 minutes before session start

**Process:**

1. **Hand out the Character Selection Guide** as players arrive
2. **Explain the 5 roles:**
   - "You're picking from one of these 5 roles. Each role has 2–3 character options. Pick the role first, then the character you like."
3. **Answer questions:**
   - "What's the difference between a Wizard and a Sorcerer?" (Wizard uses INT, prepares spells; Sorcerer uses CHA, spontaneous.)
   - "Can I play as a different race?" (No, the pre-gens are set for balance and flavor.)
4. **Assign characters:**
   - If players fight over a character, coin flip or let the faster person have it
   - Encourage players to pick different roles (ask "Is anyone picking a healer?" if you're short on support)
5. **Hand out character sheets** once selections are made
6. **5 minutes:** Let players read their sheets and ask questions

### Pre-Session Brief

Before starting, say:

> **"You're about to enter a mysterious chamber. You'll face puzzles, combat, and a final boss. The campaign is exactly 3 hours long—we're going to move fast. Don't worry about perfect rules; we'll keep it simple. Most importantly: work as a team. You're all in this together."**

## Room Setup & Ambiance

### The Soulwell Chamber Description

**Read this aloud to start:**

> *Your vision returns slowly—first light, then shape, then sound.*
>
> *You find yourselves standing inside a perfectly circular stone chamber, its surface polished smooth as glass. The air is unnervingly still… almost expectant.*
>
> *Four braziers stand evenly spaced around you, each burning with a steady blue-white flame. The fire gives off no heat. Instead, it pulses faintly, like a slowed heartbeat—its rhythm just slightly out of sync with your own.*
>
> *At the center of the room lies a large pentagram, carved with flawless, almost obsessive precision. Five unfamiliar sigils are etched into the stone around it, but only one emits light: a soft, rhythmic golden pulse, rising and dimming like slow breathing.*
>
> *The remaining four sigils sit dark and dormant, waiting their turn.*
>
> *As you shift your weight, you notice something strange—your footsteps echo half a heartbeat late, as if an invisible presence is walking just behind you.*
>
> *The temperature of the chamber wavers subtly. A passing flicker of fear cools the air; a moment of resolve warms it again. The room is reacting to you.*
>
> *There are no doors, no seams, no windows. Every inch of wall and ceiling is an unbroken surface. Yet when the brazier-light catches the stone just right, you see thousands of tiny inscriptions—etched by dozens of different hands. Prayers. Warnings. Names. All carved by those who stood here before you.*
>
> *Then, as your eyes rise toward the ceiling, the stone above seems to distort—not breaking, but bending like a mirage. A shimmering haze ripples across its surface, vanishing as quickly as it appears.*
>
> *The chamber waits. And whatever brought you here is far from finished with you.*

**After reading, add:**

> "Take a moment to look around and ask questions. What do you notice? What do you do?"

### Room Features During Puzzles

**The braziers:** Always blue-white, steady. During Puzzle 4 combat, they dim to half-light for atmosphere.

**The pentagram:** Glows gold as sigils activate. Gets brighter with each puzzle completed.

**The inscriptions:** These are reminders that others have come here. Don't dwell on them, but mention them occasionally for flavor.

**The ceiling/Soulwell:** Always visible but distant. It pulses faster as riddles are answered. Players feel its presence throughout.

**Temperature:** Fluctuates subtly based on room mood. Cold = fear/danger. Warm = resolve/progress.

**Echoes:** Footsteps always echo half a heartbeat late. This is unsettling and reinforces the "tethered soul" concept.

## Campaign Phases

### Phase 1: Opening (0:00 – 0:10)

**Goal:** Establish atmosphere and introduce the Soulwell concept

**What Happens:**
1. Read the opening description
2. Answer immediate questions ("Can I break the walls?" No. "Is there anything to eat?" No. "Can I leave?" Not yet.)
3. Introduce the mysterious voice (if players are attentive): "The chamber is watching"
4. Ask for brief character introductions (1–2 sentences each)

**DC 18 Arcana Check (Optional):**  
If a player asks about magical auras or magical theory, allow a DC 18 Arcana check:
- **Success:** They sense a Soulwell suspended above, tethering them. The sensation is faint but unmistakable.
- **Failure:** They sense magic, but can't pinpoint it.

This is optional and doesn't block any puzzles. It's just flavor.

**Ending State:** Players are in the chamber. They're ready for Puzzle 1.

---

### Phase 2: Puzzle 1 – Height Ordering (0:10 – 0:35)

**Goal:** Introduce the puzzle mechanic and establish height as a thematic element

**Key Mechanic:** The five mirrors require players to stand in height order (shortest to tallest). This height order will be important for Puzzles 3 and the final boss.

**How to Run:**

1. **Read the narration** from `puzzle1.md` (2–3 minutes)
2. **Describe the five mirrors** — each has a different arrangement of bones
3. **Players investigate** (5 minutes):
   - DC 10 Investigation/Medicine/Perception check to identify the correct mirror
   - Multiple players can check different mirrors
   - If they struggle, remind them: "Look for anatomical accuracy"
   - Once they identify the correct mirror, announce it
4. **Height ordering** (3 minutes):
   - "Stand in height order before this mirror—shortest to tallest"
   - Players arrange themselves
   - As they get in position, describe the golden beam rising from each sigil
   - If they get the order wrong, all beams vanish. They try again.
   - Once correct, all five beams ignite in unison
5. **Describe the transition** (2 minutes):
   - Mirrors melt into golden vapor
   - First sigil turns emerald green
   - Second sigil begins its golden breathing pulse
   - Read: "The chamber breathes again. One trial is complete. Another awaits."

**Common Issues:**

**Q: "Can I use magic to identify the mirror?"**  
A: Yes, but it's not necessary. A simple Investigation check works. Magic doesn't reveal anything extra.

**Q: "What if two people have the same height?"**  
A: Slightly different. One is taller by an inch. Let them check or just decide (flip a coin if needed).

**Q: "What happens if we get the order wrong?"**  
A: The beams vanish. All sigils go dark. The mirrors reset. You try again. (This can happen up to 3 times before you just give them the answer.)

**Q: "Can we remember the height order for later?"**  
A: Yes! They should notice that this order will matter in Puzzle 3. Reward attentive players.

### Phase 3: Puzzle 2 – Reflections (0:35 – 1:10)

**Goal:** Combat encounter with personal/thematic resonance

**Key Mechanic:** Each player fights a weakened reflection of themselves at Level 1. The reflection mimics their fighting style but is beatable.

**How to Run:**

1. **Read the narration** from `puzzle2.md` (1–2 minutes)
2. **Describe each reflection** briefly as it appears:
   - "Misha, before you stands yourself—but younger, less experienced, held together by golden cracks..."
   - "Thorne, your reflection wields a longsword and regards you with confused eyes..."
3. **Combat begins:**
   - Roll initiative (PCs only; reflections use fixed initiative based on DEX)
   - Run 5 simultaneous 1v1 combats
   - Each round should take 3–4 minutes total
   - Describe shatter deaths evocatively but briefly
4. **Once all reflections shattered** (2 minutes):
   - Second sigil turns emerald
   - Third sigil awakens
   - Read: "The echoes of yourselves fade into golden dust. The chamber trembles. The next trial awaits."

**Combat Pacing Tips:**

- **Use a round timer.** Say "30 seconds per person to decide your action."
- **Encourage quick decisions.** "You attack the reflection." Good. Don't deliberate for 2 minutes.
- **Describe reflection deaths in 1–2 sentences.** Don't linger.
- **If a player is close to death,** their reflection might hesitate (flavor). This is optional.
- **If the party is dominating,** reflection HP is already low. Combat should end by round 5.

**Common Issues:**

**Q: "Can I negotiate with my reflection?"**  
A: No. The reflection fights until shattered. It doesn't speak.

**Q: "What if I try to Help another player's reflection?"**  
A: Sure! Your reflection is confused by this (it expects you to fight yours). This reflects the "mirrored movement" flavor. Go for it.

**Q: "Does my reflection use my spells?"**  
A: Yes. Your reflection is a Level 1 version of your class, with the same spells/abilities you have at Level 1.

### Phase 4: Puzzle 3 – Elemental Wands (1:10 – 1:30)

**Goal:** Introduce elemental magic and reinforce the height order mechanic

**Key Mechanic:** Five wands correspond to the height order from Puzzle 1. Players must figure out which wand is theirs (by testing) and cast cantrips on sigils in the correct sequence.

**How to Run:**

1. **Read the narration** from `puzzle3.md` (1–2 minutes)
2. **Describe the five wands** (1 minute):
   - Spirit (white glow) – Light cantrip
   - Earth (green) – Mold Earth cantrip
   - Fire (red) – Fire Bolt cantrip
   - Water (blue) – Shape Water cantrip
   - Air (silver) – Gust cantrip
3. **Players pick up wands and test them** (5–7 minutes):
   - When a player picks up their wand: "It warms to your touch. The runes flare with color. It feels right."
   - When they pick up the wrong wand: "It flickers uncertainly. Sparks jump. The magic seems... resistant."
   - They learn by trial and error
4. **Casting sequence** (7–10 minutes):
   - Once they understand the assignment, ask: "Who goes first?"
   - Remind them: "The order from Puzzle 1—shortest to tallest—is the order you need."
   - Each player casts their cantrip on their sigil
   - **If correct order:** Each sigil pulses gold, then the next one awakens
   - **If wrong order:** All sigils go dark. Wands become inert. The mystery voice whispers: "Order... order... order..." They try again.
5. **Transition** (2 minutes):
   - All five sigils ignite in unison
   - Beams erupt upward like dissipating fireworks
   - Wands dissolve into golden dust
   - Third sigil turns emerald
   - Fourth sigil awakens
   - Read: "The wands are gone. The chamber trembles again. One final test remains before the guardian awakens."

**Cantrip Descriptions (Read These):**

- **Light:** The sigil glows. Soft white light spreads across the chamber.
- **Mold Earth:** The sigil rumbles. Stone dust swirls around its ring.
- **Fire Bolt:** Flame scorches the sigil. It pulses red-orange.
- **Shape Water:** A brief shimmer of water encircles the sigil. It glows blue.
- **Gust:** Wind swirls around the sigil. It glows silver and flickers.

**Common Issues:**

**Q: "Can I cast a different cantrip?"**  
A: The wand only grants the specific cantrip associated with it. You must use that cantrip.

**Q: "What if I forget the height order?"**  
A: Remind them gently: "The order from Puzzle 1—who was shortest? Who was tallest?"

**Q: "Can I cast multiple times?"**  
A: Each wand casts once per puzzle. After the cantrip is cast on a sigil, the next sigil awakens and the next player goes.

### Phase 5: Puzzle 4 – Sand Combat (1:30 – 2:05)

**Goal:** Pure combat endurance test with environmental flavor

**Key Mechanic:** Two creatures (Sand Guardian + Scarab Swarm) emerge from the ceiling as sand. Combat is straightforward—no puzzle mechanics, just survival and teamwork.

**How to Run:**

1. **Read the narration** from `puzzle4.md` (1–2 minutes)
2. **Describe the creatures forming** (1 minute)
3. **Combat begins:**
   - Roll initiative
   - Run combat for 5–6 rounds (20–25 minutes)
   - Sand Guardian uses Slam and Sand Spray
   - Scarab Swarm uses Bite and climbs on boots
   - Emphasize water vulnerability (if Shape Water was cast, remind them it deals double damage)
4. **Once both creatures defeated** (2 minutes):
   - Fourth sigil turns emerald
   - Fifth sigil awakens
   - Read: "The sand rises into the Soulwell above. The creatures dissolve. Silence returns. The final sigil glows. The final trial awaits."

**Combat Pacing Tips:**

- **Keep rounds fast.** Each round should take 3–4 minutes.
- **Describe sand movement evocatively but quickly.** "The sand snakes along the crack toward Misha's feet."
- **Remind players of creature abilities.** "The Guardian raises its arms for a slam attack."
- **If combat is dragging,** reduce enemy HP slightly. No one wants to spend 30+ minutes on Puzzle 4.

**Resource Recovery (Optional, 2 minutes):**

After Puzzle 4, before Puzzle 5, allow a brief breather:

> "You have a moment to catch your breath before the final trial. Spend a Hit Die to heal if you want. You have a few seconds."

This is optional but recommended for pacing.

**Common Issues:**

**Q: "Can I climb on the Sand Guardian?"**  
A: Sure! You climb on it. Make an Athletics or Acrobatics check (DC 12). On success, you gain advantage on your next melee attack. On failure, you fall prone.

**Q: "Does the swarm stay together or split?"**  
A: It can split across multiple PCs. Each PC it's swarming takes a Bite attack.

**Q: "What if a creature uses Sand Spray?"**  
A: All creatures in the 15-foot cone must make a CON save (DC 14) or be blinded for 1 minute.

---

### Phase 6: Puzzle 5 – Riddles (2:05 – 2:20)

**Goal:** Introduce the riddle mechanic before the final boss

**Key Mechanic:** The first two riddles are posed here, using sand and shells to form words. The remaining three riddles come during the final boss fight.

**How to Run:**

1. **Read the narration** from `puzzle5.md` (2 minutes) — describes sand and shells forming words
2. **Pose Riddle 1 (Air)** (4 minutes):
   - Read the riddle aloud
   - All PCs make DC 16 INT save or lose their next action
   - They have 2–3 minutes to discuss and answer
   - If correct: air sigil flashes, all PCs gain +10 ft movement for 2 rounds
   - If incorrect: Kaelthys heals 15 HP, party takes 4 psychic damage
3. **Pose Riddle 2 (Water)** (4 minutes):
   - Same mechanic
   - Correct: water sigil flashes, all PCs gain fire resistance for 2 rounds
   - Incorrect: Kaelthys heals, party takes damage
4. **Transition to final boss** (1 minute):
   - Read: "The sand falls silent. The words dissolve. And from the pentagram itself, a massive form rises..."
   - Kaelthys manifests

**Riddle Tips:**

- **Don't wait forever for answers.** After 2 minutes of silence, say: "Anyone?"
- **Accept close answers.** If they say "breeze" instead of "wind," that's close enough—give it to them.
- **Write down the riddle somewhere visible** so players can refer back to it.
- **If they get stuck,** offer a hint: "Think about something you can hear but not see."

**Common Issues:**

**Q: "Can I check Intelligence (Arcana) to know the riddle answer?"**  
A: No. Riddles require thinking and discussion, not rolls. Anyone can answer; no check needed.

**Q: "Can I help another player answer?"**  
A: Yes! Riddle-solving is a team activity. Discuss and collaborate.

**Q: "What if we split and some people answer differently?"**  
A: Use the first answer spoken. If it's correct, everyone benefits. If incorrect, the DM counts it as an incorrect answer and the boss heals.

---

### Phase 7: Final Boss – Kaelthys (2:20 – 3:00)

**Goal:** Climactic boss fight combining combat and riddles

**Key Mechanic:** Kaelthys fights while posing riddles. Correct riddle answers damage him and grant buffs; wrong answers heal him and hurt the party.

**How to Run:**

1. **Read Kaelthys's awakening narration** (1 minute) from `enemies/05-kaelthys.md`
2. **Combat begins:**
   - Roll initiative
   - Round 1: Kaelthys poses Riddle 3 (Earth), then attacks
   - Round 2: Kaelthys poses Riddle 4 (Fire), then attacks
   - Round 3+: Kaelthys poses Riddle 5 (Spirit) or attacks normally
   - Combat continues until Kaelthys reaches 0 HP (6–8 rounds = 25–35 minutes)
3. **Once Kaelthys reaches 0 HP:**
   - Determine victory condition:
     - **3+ riddles correct:** Clean victory. Sigils ignite. Soulwell collapses. Kaelthys banished.
     - **<3 riddles correct:** Pyrrhic victory. Partial collapse. One PC suffers consequence.
   - Read the victory text

**Combat Pacing:**

- **Riddles add time but are part of the tension.** Don't rush them.
- **Each round of combat + riddle: 4–5 minutes.**
  - 30 sec: Kaelthys poses riddle
  - 2 min: Party discusses answer
  - 1 min: Player action
  - 30 sec: Kaelthys attacks
- **Riddle 5 (Spirit) is hard.** It's okay if they don't solve it. The victory condition is 3+, not 5/5.

**Riddle Mechanics During Boss Fight:**

- **Kaelthys asks one riddle per turn** (or skips to attack)
- **DC 16 INT save for all PCs** when riddle is posed
  - Failure: lose next action
  - Success: act normally
  - BUT: if someone answers correctly, all PCs succeed and Kaelthys takes 10 radiant damage
- **Correct answer effects:**
  - Riddle 3 (Earth): +level temporary HP for all PCs
  - Riddle 4 (Fire): +1d6 radiant damage on next weapon/spell attack for all PCs
  - Riddle 5 (Spirit): advantage on all rolls for 1 round, Kaelthys vulnerable, Kaelthys staggered

**Kaelthys's Legendary Actions:**

- **Move:** Fly/walk 20 ft
- **Claw Attack:** +6 to hit, 13 damage + 3 psychic
- **Mind Glimmer:** DC 15 WIS save or stunned until end of turn
- **Soul Snap (2 actions):** DC 15 WIS save or 10 psychic damage + frightened
- **Sandstorm Blink (2 actions):** Teleport 40 ft, gain half cover
- **Riddle Interruption (3 actions):** Pose a riddle

**Common Issues:**

**Q: "Can I silence Kaelthys to stop the riddles?"**  
A: The riddles are telepathic and echoing. Silence doesn't work. Riddles are unavoidable.

**Q: "What if we run out of time?"**  
A: Reduce Kaelthys's HP or have him suddenly decide to flee. Or fade to black and resolve the rest via epilogue.

**Q: "Can we try to negotiate with Kaelthys?"**  
A: He's not interested. He wants to test them. Combat is inevitable.

## Combat Tips

### General Combat Rules

Use these simplified D&D 5e rules for this campaign:

1. **Initiative:** d20 + DEX modifier
2. **Attack Roll:** d20 + proficiency bonus + relevant ability modifier vs. AC
3. **Damage:** Roll appropriate die + ability modifier (capped at +5 for new players)
4. **AC:** Represents how hard you are to hit (no armor = 10, light = 11–15, medium = 13–17, heavy = 16–18)
5. **HP:** Health points. When reduced to 0, creature falls unconscious.
6. **Saving Throw:** d20 + relevant ability modifier (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, or CHA) vs. spell DC or effect DC

### Speeding Up Combat

1. **Use pre-rolled monster damage.** Instead of rolling for reflection attacks, use the average: Longsword = 6 damage (instead of rolling 1d8 + 2). Announce: "Your reflection's longsword hits for 6 damage."

2. **Simplify enemy abilities.** Reflections don't use bonus actions? Great, they just attack once.

3. **One round = one pass through initiative.** PC 1 acts, Enemy 1 acts, PC 2 acts, Enemy 2 acts, etc. Move fast.

4. **Set a time limit per turn.** "30 seconds to decide your action, or you Dodge."

5. **Use the same music throughout combat.** It sets the tone and moves things along.

### Managing Multiple Combats

**Puzzle 2 (5 simultaneous 1v1s):**
- Run each player's combat in sequence, not in parallel
- "Misha, what do you do?" (Misha acts, reflection acts.)
- "Thorne, what do you do?" (Thorne acts, reflection acts.)
- Repeat for rounds
- This keeps momentum and prevents long waits

**Puzzle 4 (2 creatures vs. 5 PCs):**
- Players act in initiative order
- Sand Guardian acts (attacks someone or uses ability)
- Scarab Swarm acts (climbs on someone or attacks)
- Repeat
- This is standard 5e combat

### Monster HP Management

If combat is taking too long:
- Reduce monster HP by 10% each round
- Or reduce HP before the fight starts
- Or have a creature flee/surrender at ½ HP

If combat is too easy:
- Add more creatures
- Give monsters bonus actions or reactions
- Have creatures use spells/abilities every turn

## Riddle Mechanics

### How Riddles Work in This Campaign

**Riddle-Solving is a Party Activity**

- Any PC can answer a riddle
- PCs can discuss and debate
- One answer is given (the first one spoken)
- If correct, all PCs benefit
- If incorrect, the party suffers consequences

### The Five Riddles

| # | Element | Difficulty | Answer | Correct Reward |
|---|---------|------------|--------|---|
| 1 | Air | Easy | Wind/Air | +10 ft movement (2 rounds) |
| 2 | Water | Moderate | Water/River | Resist fire damage (2 rounds) |
| 3 | Earth | Challenging | Mountain/Earth/Rock | Temporary HP (per level) |
| 4 | Fire | Hard | Fire/Flame | +1d6 radiant damage (next attack) |
| 5 | Spirit | Very Hard | Name/True Name | Advantage all rolls (1 round) + Kaelthys vulnerable + staggered |

### Accepting Answers

**Be flexible. These are riddles, not trivia.**

**Riddle 1 (Wind):**
- Accept: Wind, Air, Breeze, Gust (anything moving air)
- Reject: Breath, Voice, Sound, Music

**Riddle 2 (Water):**
- Accept: Water, River, Stream, Rain, Waterfall (anything flowing water)
- Reject: Ocean (too big, doesn't "fall"), Dew (doesn't flow), Tears

**Riddle 3 (Mountain):**
- Accept: Mountain, Earth, Rock, Stone, Hill, Peak (anything stable and ancient)
- Reject: Castle, Wall, Fortress (made by people, not ancient enough)

**Riddle 4 (Fire):**
- Accept: Fire, Flame, Blaze, Heat (anything burning)
- Reject: Sun, Lightning, Passion (not literally fire)

**Riddle 5 (Name):**
- Accept: Name, True Name, Word, Language (anything that identifies)
- Reject: Signature, Identity, Label (too specific or modern)

### If the Party Gets Stuck

**After 2 minutes of silence, offer a hint:**

1. **Hint 1:** Restate the riddle
2. **Hint 2:** Give a contextual clue
3. **Hint 3:** Give the answer

**Riddle 1 (Wind) – Hints:**
1. "It's something natural."
2. "Something you can feel but not see."
3. "Wind or Air."

**Riddle 2 (Water) – Hints:**
1. "It's about something liquid."
2. "Think about rivers and how they shape the land."
3. "Water."

**Riddle 3 (Earth) – Hints:**
1. "It's something that stands for a very, very long time."
2. "Think about something humans cannot build, but nature creates."
3. "Mountain or Earth."

**Riddle 4 (Fire) – Hints:**
1. "It's something that can be destructive and helpful at the same time."
2. "Think about something that burns and gives light."
3. "Fire."

**Riddle 5 (Spirit) – Hints:**
1. "It's a concept, not a physical thing."
2. "Think about identity, language, power, magic."
3. "A Name or True Name."

## Difficulty Scaling

### Adjusting for Party Strength

**If the Party is Struggling:**

**Puzzle 1:** Allow DC 12 Investigation instead of DC 10. Give hints freely.

**Puzzle 2 (Reflections):**
- Reduce reflection HP by 3–5 each
- Reflections act after PCs each round
- Reflections don't use bonus action abilities

**Puzzle 3 (Wands):**
- After 1 wrong attempt, tell them the order (don't make them guess twice)

**Puzzle 4 (Sand Combat):**
- Reduce Sand Guardian HP to 16
- Reduce Scarab Swarm HP to 12
- Reduce damage of Sand Spray to 1d4

**Final Boss (Kaelthys):**
- Reduce HP to 60

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Time Management

**Problem:** You're running behind schedule.

**Solution:**
1. Skip elaborate descriptions. Stick to essentials.
2. Reduce puzzle investigation time. Give answers sooner.
3. Reduce combat round count. Have creatures surrender or flee.
4. Skip riddle discussion. Just give the answers.
5. Cut the epilogue (fade to black instead).

**Problem:** You're ahead of schedule.

**Solution:**
1. Add more riddle banter. Let players discuss longer.
2. Extend combat. Have enemies use more abilities.
3. Describe rooms and creatures in more detail.
4. Add a short quest-hook epilogue.

### Player Confusion

**Problem:** Players don't understand the height order mechanic.

**Solution:** Explain it simply: "Stand shortest to tallest, in the same order you stood in Puzzle 1."

**Problem:** Players are confused about which character is tallest/shortest.

**Solution:** Have them physically stand up and look, or assign heights (5'6", 6'1", etc.). Use character height from the pre-gen sheet.

**Problem:** Players don't understand how riddles work.

**Solution:** Explain before the first riddle: "I'll pose a riddle. You get to discuss and answer. If your answer is correct, you all benefit. If not, the boss heals. You can guess once per riddle."

### Combat Issues

**Problem:** Combat is taking too long.

**Solution:** Reduce monster HP, simplify abilities, use pre-rolled damage, set a timer per turn.

**Problem:** A PC is dying.

**Solution:** Have an enemy use a less lethal action. Or allow a PC to use a healing ability (most healers have spells). Or reduce enemy damage slightly.

**Problem:** A reflection is winning its 1v1.

**Solution:** Have the PC's reflection hesitate (flavor), or reduce reflection HP.

### Riddle Issues

**Problem:** Nobody knows the answer.

**Solution:** Give a hint after 2 minutes. Give the answer if they're stuck after another minute.

**Problem:** The party keeps guessing wrong.

**Solution:** This is intentional. Wrong answers have consequences. The party can still win with 3+ correct answers. Encourage them but don't give free answers.

**Problem:** Riddle 5 is too hard and the party is stuck.

**Solution:** Offer hints or give the answer. They still win if they get 3+ total, so it's not critical to get Riddle 5.

### Other Issues

**Problem:** A player wants to do something not covered in the rules.

**Solution:** Decide quickly and move on. Say: "Sure, make a [relevant check]" and keep going. Don't pause for 5 minutes to debate mechanics.

**Problem:** A player is disruptive or not engaged.

**Solution:** Involve them directly: "Hey, it's your turn. What do you do?" If they're being disruptive, talk to them during a break.

**Problem:** The party refuses to engage with a puzzle or riddle.

**Solution:** Remind them: "This is mandatory. The chamber won't let you skip." Keep momentum. Force them forward.

## Ending & Epilogue

### Victory Condition Determination

Track riddles answered correctly during Puzzle 5 and the final boss fight:

**3+ Correct Riddles = Clean Victory**

> The five emerald sigils ignite at once. Beams of emerald light erupt upward from the pentagram, lashing around Kaelthys like chains. His form contorts—shredded by the power he once commanded. The Soulwell above collapses inward with a deafening roar.
>
> The tether snaps. You feel it break inside you—a sudden, profound severance. You are free.
>
> Kaelthys dissolves into silver-gold motes of light. They spiral upward and vanish into the collapsing Soulwell. His final words echo in your minds:
>
> *"Even shattered… I am never truly gone. A riddle unanswered waits forever."*
>
> The pentagram cracks. The chamber trembles. And a passage opens where there was none before—a way out.

**<3 Correct Riddles = Pyrrhic Victory**

> Kaelthys's death triggers a partial collapse. The chamber shudders violently. The remaining sigils dim, their power spent.
>
> The Soulwell above convulses, then stabilizes. It has lost its guardian, but its hunger remains.
>
> Kaelthys dissolves into silver-gold motes, but they don't vanish completely. Some cling to you—invisible threads that feel like they won't fully break.
>
> One of you feels it most keenly [choose a PC at random]. A cold sensation, a faint whisper, a presence that shouldn't be there. The Soulwell's grip has loosened, but it has marked you.
>
> A passage opens. You stumble out into cold air and starlight. You're alive. You're free.
>
> But something follows you. Something that shouldn't be possible.

**Consequences for <3 Riddles (Pick One):**

1. **Exhaustion:** One PC gains one level of Exhaustion that lasts until they complete a long rest (and even then, it lingers as a feeling).
2. **Permanent HP Loss:** One PC loses 1 maximum HP (permanent reminder of the Soulwell's touch).
3. **Curse:** One PC is cursed. Disadvantage on one ability until they complete a quest to lift it.

### Memory Resolution (Optional)

**If Time Allows (5–10 minutes):**

After escaping, the party experiences a brief scene where their memories of the Soulwell Chamber start to fade or remain vivid.

**Option A: Memories Fade**

> As you walk into the night, the memory of the chamber becomes... distant. Not gone, but like a dream upon waking. You remember the fear, the puzzles, the sphinx. But the details slip away like water through your fingers.
>
> By tomorrow morning, you'll remember this happened. But the specifics—the riddles, the names of the sigils, the exact look of Kaelthys's face—will be hazy.
>
> It's as if the Soulwell is erasing your memory of it, even in defeat. A final insult. A final mystery.

**Option B: Memories Remain**

> The night air clears your mind. Strangely, the memories don't fade. They remain vivid—perhaps too vivid. Every detail of the Soulwell Chamber, every riddle, every moment of combat is burned into your memory with perfect clarity.
>
> This is your victory. You will never forget what happened. The Soulwell could not erase you.

### Epilogue (2–3 minutes)

After victory is determined, ask the party:

> "As you exit the chamber and step into the world beyond, what do you do? How do you process what just happened? Take a moment to describe your character's reaction."

Let each player say a brief (1–2 sentence) epilogue thought. Then wrap up:

> "You've survived the Soulwell Chamber. Whether your memories fade or remain sharp, you carry this experience with you. The riddles, the puzzles, the sphinx—they're part of you now. You're alive. You made it."

### After the Session

1. **Check in with the party:** "How did you feel about the campaign? Any feedback?"
2. **Memory resolution:** If not done in-session, send a brief note about whether memories fade or remain.
3. **Thank players:** Thank them for a great session.
4. **Optional sequel:** If players want more, hint at a follow-up campaign involving the Soulwell's origins.
