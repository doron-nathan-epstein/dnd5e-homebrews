# The Soulwell Chamber: 3-Hour Pacing Guide

**Session Length:** 3 hours (180 minutes)  
**Time Conversion:** 3 minutes real-time = 1 minute in-game  
**In-Game Duration:** ~3 hours

---

## Overview

This guide breaks down the entire campaign into time blocks. **Stick to these timings to keep the session on track.** The campaign is designed to fit exactly in 3 hours with zero downtime.

Key principle: **Keep momentum.** Don't linger on descriptions or player deliberation. Read situations concisely and move forward.

---

## Timeline

### **0:00 – 0:10 | Opening (10 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber  
**What Happens:** Players arrive and read the opening description

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the opening narration from `campaign.md` (about 3 minutes)
2. Describe the room and braziers
3. Answer immediate questions about surroundings
4. Introduce the five dormant sigils and the glowing one
5. Ask players to introduce their characters briefly (1–2 sentences each)

**Timing Notes:**
- Don't over-describe. Stick to the script.
- If players ask about climbing walls or testing the chamber, say "the walls are smooth and seamless; nothing gives."
- The DC 18 Arcana check for sensing the Soulwell is optional. Only offer if someone asks about magical auras.

**End State:** Players understand they're in a strange chamber, tethered to something above, and that puzzles await.

---

### **0:10 – 0:35 | Puzzle 1: Hall of Ordered Form (25 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber (same room)  
**What Happens:** Five mirrors appear. Players figure out which is correct and line up by height.

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the puzzle narration (2 minutes) — describes mirrors materializing and the mysterious voice
2. Ask players to examine the mirrors (5 minutes) — they investigate and make Investigation/Medicine/Perception checks (DC 10)
3. Guide them to the correct mirror (quick, once identified)
4. Announce the height lineup requirement
5. Have players physically arrange by height (3 minutes) — move around the table or verbally establish order
6. Apply the golden glow/beam feedback as they get in position (3 minutes)
7. Once correct: describe mirrors melting, first sigil turning emerald, second sigil awakening (2 minutes)

**Timing Tips:**
- If players are slow to identify the mirror, give a hint after 3 minutes: "The correct mirror shows the bones in proper anatomical order"
- Height ordering should be quick and fun—don't get bogged down
- One failed attempt is fine; if they fail 3+ times, just give them the answer to move forward

**End State:** First sigil is emerald. Second sigil is glowing. Puzzle 2 is ready to begin.

---

### **0:35 – 1:10 | Puzzle 2: Hall of Shattered Selves (35 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber  
**What Happens:** Five reflections manifest. Combat begins.

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the puzzle narration (1 minute) — describes golden vapors forming reflections
2. Describe each player's reflection briefly (30 seconds per reflection, 2.5 minutes total)
3. Roll initiative for reflections (1 minute)
4. Run combat (25 minutes) — each round should take about 3–4 minutes:
   - Player 1 takes action
   - Reflection 1 attacks
   - Player 2 takes action
   - Reflection 2 attacks
   - (etc. for all 5)
5. Once all reflections shattered: describe second sigil turning emerald, third sigil awakening (2 minutes)

**Combat Pacing:**
- **Round 1:** Players feel out the reflections. Reflections are simple and attack once.
- **Rounds 2–3:** Players focus fire and start killing reflections. Reflections use bonus actions.
- **Rounds 4–5:** Reflections die, combat ends.
- **Target combat length:** 4–5 rounds = 12–20 minutes of actual combat time

**Timing Tips:**
- Don't let players debate tactics for more than 30 seconds per turn
- Describe reflection deaths evocatively (1–2 sentences each) but quickly
- Once one reflection is shattered, enemies start feeling "desperate" — they might flee (but never actually do)

**End State:** All reflections destroyed. Second sigil is emerald. Third sigil is glowing. Puzzle 3 is ready.

---

### **1:10 – 1:30 | Puzzle 3: Hall of Elemental Remains (20 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber  
**What Happens:** Five wands manifest. Players must figure out which wand is theirs and cast cantrips on sigils in correct order.

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the puzzle narration (1 minute) — describes dust forming bones, bones becoming wands
2. Describe the five wands (2 minutes) — quick, evocative descriptions of each element
3. Players pick up wands and test them (5 minutes):
   - They figure out which wand is theirs (rejected vs. accepted by the wand)
   - Encourage them to try and learn through feedback
4. Announce the casting sequence (1 minute) — "Cast in the same order you stood in Puzzle 1"
5. Players cast their cantrips on the sigils in sequence (8 minutes):
   - Each casting takes about 30–60 seconds (describe the effect)
   - Wrong order resets everything (if this happens, give them a gentle hint about the height order)
   - Correct order triggers all 5 beams at once
6. Describe third sigil turning emerald, fourth sigil awakening (2 minutes)

**Timing Tips:**
- If players forget the height order, remind them: "The order from Puzzle 1 — shortest to tallest"
- One reset is fine; if they reset twice, just tell them the order to move forward
- Describe each cantrip effect quickly: "Fire Bolt scorches the Fire sigil. It pulses gold."

**End State:** Third sigil is emerald. Fourth sigil is glowing. Puzzle 4 is ready.

---

### **1:30 – 2:05 | Puzzle 4: Hall of Shifting Dust (35 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber  
**What Happens:** Sand Elemental and Scarab Swarm emerge. Combat.

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the puzzle narration (1 minute) — describes sand falling and creatures forming
2. Announce the enemies (Sand Guardian, Scarab Swarm)
3. Roll initiative (1 minute)
4. Run combat (30 minutes):
   - This is a pure endurance fight with no puzzle mechanics
   - Use the same pacing as Puzzle 2 (each round 3–4 minutes)
   - Describe the sand moving unnaturally (extra flavor)
5. Once both creatures defeated: describe fourth sigil turning emerald, fifth sigil awakening (2 minutes)

**Combat Pacing:**
- **Round 1–2:** Players learn enemy abilities. Sand Guardian uses Slam, Scarabs climb onto boots.
- **Round 3–5:** Creatures use more abilities (Sand Spray, Swarm Attack). Players focus fire.
- **Round 6+:** Combat ends as creatures drop to 0 HP.
- **Target combat length:** 5–6 rounds = 20–25 minutes

**Timing Tips:**
- Don't describe every sand movement in detail. Keep descriptions punchy.
- If players are doing really well, end early (don't drag it out)
- If players are struggling, emphasize water/bludgeoning as effective damage types as hints

**Resource Recovery (Optional):**
- Between Puzzle 4 and Puzzle 5, allow 2 minutes of "catch your breath" time
- Players can spend Hit Dice to heal
- DM: "You have a moment to recover before the final trial"

**End State:** Fourth sigil is emerald. Fifth sigil is glowing. Puzzle 5 (riddles) + Final Boss are ready.

---

### **2:05 – 2:20 | Puzzle 5: The Riddle of the Sphinx (15 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber  
**What Happens:** Sand forms words. Kaelthys poses riddles.

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the puzzle narration (2 minutes) — describes sand forming words, mysterious voice echoing
2. Pose Riddle 1 (Air) (1 minute):
   - Players make INT saves or lose next action
   - They discuss and answer (3–4 minutes)
3. Pose Riddle 2 (Water) (1 minute):
   - Same mechanic (3–4 minutes for discussion + answer)

**Timing Notes:**
- Don't wait forever for answers. After 2 minutes of silence, ask: "Anyone have a guess?"
- Correct answers grant rewards; move quickly to acknowledge them
- Wrong answers: boss heals, party takes damage, move to next riddle
- **Only 2 riddles in this phase**. The remaining 3 riddles come during the final boss fight.

**End State:** Kaelthys physically manifests. Final boss combat begins.

---

### **2:20 – 3:00 | Final Boss: Kaelthys, the Shifting Riddle (40 minutes)**

**Location:** The Soulwell Chamber  
**What Happens:** Epic combat with integrated riddles.

**DM Actions:**
1. Read the awakening narration (1 minute) — Kaelthys manifests, introduces himself
2. Roll initiative (1 minute)
3. Run combat (35 minutes):
   - **Round 1:** Kaelthys poses Riddle 3 (Earth), then attacks
   - **Round 2:** Kaelthys poses Riddle 4 (Fire), then attacks
   - **Round 3+:** Kaelthys poses Riddle 5 (Spirit) or uses actions to attack
   - Combat continues with Kaelthys using legendary actions (Claw, Soul Snap, Sandstorm Blink)
4. Once Kaelthys reaches 0 HP: determine victory condition (3+ riddles = clean victory, <3 riddles = pyrrhic victory)
5. Describe victory sequence (2 minutes) — sigils igniting, Soulwell collapsing, Kaelthys dissolving

**Combat Pacing:**
- Kaelthys is a solo boss, so combat is faster than multi-creature fights
- Each round should take 3–5 minutes (riddle discussion + player actions + boss attacks)
- **Target boss fight length:** 6–8 rounds = 20–30 minutes
- Actual combat with legend actions is quick; riddles add time

**Riddle Timing:**
- Riddle 3 (Earth): 2–3 minutes discussion + answer
- Riddle 4 (Fire): 2–3 minutes discussion + answer
- Riddle 5 (Spirit): 3–5 minutes discussion + answer (this one is hard)

**Timing Tips:**
- If riddle discussion is taking too long, give hints: "Think about concepts more abstract than physical objects"
- Don't wait for perfect silence. If the party is clearly stuck, offer the answer to move forward
- Prioritize combat momentum over riddle perfection. Wrong answers still move the fight forward.

**End State:** Kaelthys is defeated. Victory condition determined. Campaign effectively complete.

---

### **3:00 | End / Memory Resolution (Optional)**

**What Happens:** Narrative resolution (memory fade vs. wipe)

**Timing:** This is optional and can extend past 3 hours if time allows, or be resolved quickly if time is tight.

**DM Actions:**
1. Describe the escape from the chamber (2 minutes)
2. Resolve the memory mechanic based on riddles solved (5–10 minutes)
3. End scene / epilogue (2–3 minutes)

**Note:** If you're strictly adhering to 3 hours, you can fade to black right after Kaelthys dies and promise a brief epilogue via email or voice note later.

---

## Strict Time Management Tips

### If You're Behind Schedule

1. **Skip elaborate descriptions.** Stick to essential flavor only.
2. **Puzzle 1 taking too long?** After 15 minutes, just give them the correct mirror.
3. **Puzzle 2 combat running long?** Reduce reflection HP by 3–5 each. Finish faster.
4. **Puzzle 3 taking too long?** Tell them the height order is "shortest to tallest" immediately.
5. **Puzzle 4 running long?** Reduce enemy HP or have them surrender at ½ HP (flee into Soulwell).
6. **Final boss taking too long?** Reduce Kaelthys's HP by 10–15. Finish faster.

### If You're Ahead of Schedule

1. **Add more riddle banter.** Let players discuss longer.
2. **Extend Kaelthys combat.** Have him use more legendary actions.
3. **Add encounter epilogue.** Describe the escape and memory resolution in detail.
4. **Play out a short quest-hook.** Hint at a follow-up campaign.

## Notes for New DMs

1. **Riddles aren't optional.** Kaelthys will pose them. If the party wants to skip or ignore riddles, Kaelthys just keeps repeating them telepathically. Riddle-solving is mandatory.

2. **Time is tight.** This is a 3-hour one-shot. Every minute counts. Be willing to skip or abbreviate descriptions to stay on schedule.

3. **Combat moves fast.** Don't let players take 2 minutes per turn. Aim for 60 seconds of player decision-making, then 30 seconds of enemy action.

4. **Riddles move the story, not combat.** Wrong riddle answers are part of the narrative—the boss heals and taunts. This is intentional and pushes the story forward.

5. **Victory condition matters.** Track how many riddles the party answers correctly. This determines the ending tone (triumphant vs. bittersweet).

---

## Post-Session Notes

Once the session ends, follow up with:

1. **Memory Resolution:** If not completed in-session, resolve via email or voice note
2. **Level Up:** Remind players they reached Level 3
3. **Feedback:** Ask how they felt about the campaign (riddles, pacing, difficulty)
4. **Follow-Up:** Hint at a potential sequel if players want to continue
