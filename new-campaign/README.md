# The Soulwell Chamber

**A 3-Hour D&D 5e One-Shot for 5 Level 1–3 Players**

## Quick Start

1. **Print these files:**
   - `Characters/00-CHARACTER-SELECTION-GUIDE.md` (give to players)
   - All character sheets in `Characters/` (have 12 available)
   - `Guides/PACING.md` (for you, keep visible)

2. **Prepare:**
   - Read `Guides/DM-MASTER-GUIDE.md` completely
   - Print or reference enemy stat blocks in `Enemies/`
   - Have dice ready

3. **Run it:**
   - Seat players, have them pick characters
   - Read the opening narration in `Campaign/campaign.md`
   - Follow the 5 puzzles in order (Campaign folder)
   - End with the final boss (Kaelthys) in `Campaign/puzzle5.md`

## What's Included

### Characters (12 pre-generated)
- 1 Fighter, Barbarian, Monk, Ranger (melee/strikers)
- 1 Rogue, Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock (ranged/casters)
- 1 Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Bard (support/control)

**All stat blocks, spells, and mechanics included.** Players don't need anything but the sheets.

### Campaign (5 Puzzles + Final Boss)
1. **Puzzle 1 (Sigils)** — Arcana/Investigation puzzle. Light sigils in correct elemental order.
2. **Puzzle 2 (Reflections)** — Combat. Fight weakened versions of yourselves.
3. **Puzzle 3 (Wands)** — Elemental magic puzzle. Cast spells in correct order on sigils.
4. **Puzzle 4 (Desert)** — Combat + exploration. Sand Guardian (unkillable by melee) and Scarab Swarm.
5. **Puzzle 5 (Kaelthys)** — Final boss. Answer 5 riddles while fighting an ancient sphinx.

### Enemies (16 stat blocks)
- 8 reflection enemies (one per character class)
- 2 puzzle 4 enemies (Guardian, Swarm)
- 1 final boss (Kaelthys)

All include AC, HP, attacks, spells, behavior notes, and DM tactics.

### Guides
- **DM-MASTER-GUIDE.md** — Complete running guide with rules, pacing, difficulty scaling, contingencies
- **PACING.md** — Minute-by-minute breakdown. Keep this visible during play.

## Campaign Structure

**Total Time:** 3 hours (180 minutes)  
**Level Progression:** Start Level 1 → Level 2 after Puzzle 2 → Level 3 before Puzzle 5  
**Party Size:** Exactly 5 players (one per archetypal role)  
**Difficulty:** Medium (solvable puzzles, challenging combat, multiple victory conditions)

## Victory Conditions

- **Clean Victory (3+ riddles solved):** Kaelthys banished, party escapes with minds intact
- **Pyrrhic Victory (<3 riddles):** Party survives, one PC suffers a consequence (curse, exhaustion, lost memory)
- **Defeat:** Unlikely but possible if Kaelthys kills all PCs early

## Key Mechanics

- **Elemental Theme:** 5 elements (Air, Water, Earth, Fire, Spirit) woven through all puzzles
- **Riddle System:** 5 riddles tied to final boss; correct answers deal damage and grant temp HP; wrong answers heal boss and hurt player
- **Tether Mechanic:** PCs sense they're magically tethered to something above (the Soulwell); completing puzzles makes this clearer
- **Memory Consequences:** If party fails final riddles, they may suffer memory loss (optional variant)

## Running Tips

- **Stay on schedule:** Use PACING.md ruthlessly. If players are stuck on a puzzle, give hints after 10 minutes.
- **Combat is dangerous:** Even at Level 3, Kaelthys can kill a PC in 2–3 hits. Play the sphinx intelligently (use spells, positioning, legendary actions).
- **Encourage riddle solving:** The riddle mechanic is the core of the final fight. Reward clever interpretations.
- **Use the atmosphere:** The chamber is alive and reactive. Describe how the room responds to PC actions and emotions.
