# Campaign Overview

**Party Size:** 5 players (one per role: Melee Striker, Ranged Striker, Spellcaster, Healer/Support, Skill Expert)

**Starting Level:** 1

**Session Length:** 3 hours (180 minutes)

**In-Game Duration:** Approximately 3 hours

## Level Progression

- Start: Level 1
- After Puzzle 2 (Reflections): Advance to Level 2
- Before Puzzle 5 (Riddles): Advance to Level 3

## The Campaign

**The Soulwell Chamber** is a mysterious stone chamber where five adventurers awaken, tethered to a cosmic entity above. They must solve five progressively difficult puzzles while answering riddles posed by an ancient sphinx. Success means freedom and their minds intact; failure means the Soulwell claims their souls.

### Tone & Atmosphere

**Atmosphere:** Eerie, mysterious, with moments of wonder and dread. The chamber feels *alive* and *hungry*.

**Pacing:** Fast-moving. No downtime. Each puzzle leads directly to the next.

**Threat Level:** Medium. Puzzles are solvable; combat is challenging but survivable for a Level 1–3 party that works together.

# DM Guide Essentials

## Overview

**The Campaign in One Paragraph:**

Five adventurers awaken in a mysterious stone chamber, tethered to a cosmic entity called the Soulwell. They must solve five progressively difficult puzzles—solving riddles, fighting their own reflections, casting elemental magic, and enduring combat—while answering riddles posed by an ancient sphinx. Success means freedom; failure means the Soulwell claims their souls.

## Room Setup & Ambiance

The chamber is circular, approximately 40 feet in diameter. Four braziers stand evenly spaced, each burning with steady blue-white flame. At the center is a large pentagram with five sigils. The walls and ceiling are smooth, polished stone.

**Features:**

- **Braziers:** Blue-white, steady. Dim to half-light during Puzzle 4 combat
- **Pentagram:** Glows gold as sigils activate. Gets brighter with each puzzle
- **Inscriptions:** Thousands of tiny carvings on walls—prayers, warnings, names
- **Ceiling/Soulwell:** Always visible, distant. Pulses faster as riddles answered
- **Temperature:** Fluctuates subtly. Cold = fear/danger. Warm = resolve/progress
- **Echoes:** Footsteps echo half a heartbeat late

## Combat Tips

### General Combat Rules

1. **Initiative:** d20 + DEX
2. **Attack Roll:** d20 + proficiency + ability modifier vs. AC
3. **Damage:** Roll appropriate die + ability modifier
4. **AC:** How hard to hit (unarmored 10, light 11–15, medium 13–17, heavy 16–18)
5. **HP:** Health points. At 0, creature falls unconscious
6. **Saving Throw:** d20 + ability modifier vs. spell DC

### Speeding Up Combat

1. Use pre-rolled monster damage (average instead of roll)
2. Simplify enemy abilities
3. Set a time limit per turn ("30 seconds to decide")
4. Use consistent background music
5. Run simultaneous 1v1s in sequence, not parallel

## Difficulty Scaling

### If Party is Struggling

- **Puzzle 1:** Allow DC 12 Investigation, give hints freely
- **Puzzle 2:** Reduce reflection HP by 3–5, reflections act after PCs
- **Puzzle 3:** After 1 wrong attempt, tell them the order
- **Puzzle 4:** Reduce Sand Guardian HP to 16, Scarab Swarm to 12
- **Final Boss:** Reduce HP to 60, riddle DC to 14, incorrect healing to 10

### If Party is Dominating

- **Puzzle 1:** Increase DC to 13
- **Puzzle 2:** Increase HP by 3–5, reflections use full abilities
- **Puzzle 3:** Make wand assignment less obvious
- **Puzzle 4:** Increase Sand Guardian HP to 26, Swarm to 20, add second Swarm
- **Final Boss:** Increase HP to 85, riddle DC to 18, incorrect healing to 20

## Riddle Mechanics

### How Riddles Work

- Riddle-solving is party activity
- Any PC can answer
- PCs can discuss and debate
- One answer given (first spoken)
- If correct, all benefit
- If incorrect, party suffers consequences

### Accepting Answers

**Be flexible. These are riddles, not trivia.**

- **Riddle 1:** Wind, Air, Breeze, Gust (anything moving air)
- **Riddle 2:** Water, River, Stream, Rain (flowing water)
- **Riddle 3:** Mountain, Earth, Rock, Stone, Hill (ancient, solid things)
- **Riddle 4:** Fire, Flame, Blaze, Heat (anything burning)
- **Riddle 5:** Name, True Name, Word, Language (identifying things)

### If Party Gets Stuck

After 2 minutes: offer a hint
After 4 minutes: give the answer

# Common Problems & Solutions

## Time Management

**Problem:** Running behind schedule.

**Solution:** Skip elaborate descriptions, reduce investigation time, reduce combat rounds, skip epilogue

**Problem:** Running ahead of schedule.

**Solution:** Add riddle banter, extend combat, describe more detail, add epilogue

## Player Confusion

**Problem:** Height order mechanic unclear.

**Solution:** "Stand shortest to tallest, in the same order as Puzzle 1."

**Problem:** Don't understand riddles.

**Solution:** "I pose a riddle. You discuss and answer. If correct, you all benefit. If not, the boss heals."

## Combat Issues

**Problem:** Combat taking too long.

**Solution:** Reduce monster HP, simplify abilities, use pre-rolled damage, set timer per turn

**Problem:** Reflection winning its 1v1.

**Solution:** Have reflection hesitate (flavor) or reduce HP

## Riddle Issues

**Problem:** Nobody knows the answer.

**Solution:** Give hint after 2 minutes, answer after 4 minutes

   - Scarab Swarm tokens (small swarming miniature)

## Character Selection Process

**Time Allocation:** 10 minutes before session start

1. Hand out the **Character Selection Guide**

# Opening Narration

*Your vision returns slowly—first light, then shape, then sound.*

You find yourselves standing inside a perfectly circular stone chamber, its surface polished smooth as glass. The air is unnervingly still… almost *expectant*.

Four braziers stand evenly spaced around you, each burning with a steady blue-white flame. The fire gives off no heat. Instead, it pulses faintly, like a slowed heartbeat—its rhythm just slightly out of sync with your own.

At the center of the room lies a **large pentagram**, carved with flawless, almost obsessive precision. Five unfamiliar sigils are etched into the stone around it, but **only one** emits light: a soft, rhythmic **golden pulse**, rising and dimming like slow breathing. The remaining four sigils sit dark and dormant, waiting their turn.

As you shift your weight, you notice something strange—your footsteps echo half a heartbeat late, as if an invisible presence is walking just behind you.

The temperature of the chamber wavers subtly. A passing flicker of fear cools the air; a moment of resolve warms it again. The room is reacting to you.

There are **no doors, no seams, no windows**. Every inch of wall and ceiling is an unbroken surface. Yet when the brazier-light catches the stone just right, you see thousands of tiny inscriptions—etched by dozens of different hands.

Prayers. Warnings. Names. All carved by those who stood here before you.

Then, as your eyes rise toward the ceiling, the stone above seems to distort—not breaking, but *bending* like a mirage. A shimmering haze ripples across its surface, vanishing as quickly as it appears.

---

### Optional DC 18 Arcana Check

A character who succeeds on a **DC 18 Arcana check** senses that the distortion is not an illusion of light, but a veil—thin, unstable, and threaded with powerful magic.

With success, they glimpse what the chamber is trying to conceal:

A **Soulwell**, suspended beyond the physical plane, its form a swirling construct of shadow and luminescence turning in impossible shapes. From it, faint threads of shimmering essence descend like gossamer strands and **tether themselves to each of you**. You feel no pain, no drain… but your souls are undeniably connected to the thing above. A constant, gentle pull—like a distant inhale.

*DM Note:* With each puzzle completed, the magical veil weakens. The DC to perceive the Soulwell **decreases by 2**, eventually allowing the truth of the room to become unmistakable even to the untrained eye.

---

And then, from the pentagram beneath your feet, you feel something answer.

A slow intake of air. A subtle draw toward the glowing sigil. As though the *floor itself* has just… breathed.

The chamber waits. And whatever brought you here is far from finished with you.

# Puzzle 1: The Hall of Ordered Form

*The golden glow of the active sigil at the pentagram's edge pulses once—slow and deep—like the chamber taking a deliberate breath.*

The air around the pentagram begins to shimmer. Light bends. Shadows twist. And from thin, swirling motes of bone-dust and reflected light, **five floating mirrors materialize**, each locking into place before a point of the pentagram.

They hover there, drifting ever so slightly, as though breathing with the room itself.

Each mirror is framed in an elaborate construction of bones—**skulls, jawbones, clavicles, scapulae, humeri, ribs, spines, pelvises, femurs, kneecaps, tibias, fibulas, and toes**—all woven into grotesque, ornamental arrangements.

Yet no two mirrors are the same. Some frames spiral; others stack in impossible geometry. Some seem chaotic. Some strangely artistic.

And then, echoing from nowhere and everywhere at once, a **mysterious voice** fills the chamber:

> **"Head, shoulders, knees, and toes.**
> **Before the taller, you each must go.**
> **Reflect in height upon your visage,**
> **For in this order, on your souls I will pillage.**
> **For each of you a sigil assigned—**
> **Remember this for when on your souls I will dine.**
> **Get in line, hurry—chop chop.**
> **The clock waits for no one. Tick tock."**

## Puzzle Mechanics

### Step 1: Identifying the Correct Mirror

The players must examine the five mirrors. Although each mirror showcases all manner of skeletal pieces, **only one features the five key skeletal structures in true anatomical descending order**:

**Skull → Shoulders → Spine → Knees → Toes**

The others misplace, reverse, spiral, or break this sequence.

Players may identify the correct mirror with:

- **Investigation**, **Medicine**, or **Perception** check (DC 10)
- Or **Passive Perception 14**
- No roll needed for players who simply figure it out.

**DM Note:** Randomly determine which of the five mirrors is correct for this run (roll 1d5, or simply pick one).

### Step 2: Height Order Lineup

Once the correct mirror is found, players must **line up before it in height order**, shortest to tallest—*"before the taller you each must go."*

When a player stands in the **correct position**:

- Their matching sigil on the pentagram begins to **breathe with a soft golden glow**
- A **thin golden beam** rises vertically from the circular ring around that sigil

When a player stands in the **wrong position**:

- All breathing glows cease
- All beams vanish
- The sigils flicker and fall dark again

Once the lineup is **perfect**:

- The **first sigil** stops breathing and transforms from gold into a **vivid emerald green**
- Its beam brightens sharply, then extinguishes
- The sigil remains a **solid emerald glow**, marking the task complete
- The five mirrors **melt away**, dissolving into drifting golden vapour that evaporates into the air
- Immediately, the **second sigil clockwise** around the pentagram awakens, beginning its own soft, golden, breathing pulse—the next trial is ready

## Sigil Significance (DM-Only)

Each player effectively receives their own sigil through this puzzle. These sigils will later be crucial in the **final encounter**—either to weaken, bind, or entrap the BBEG. The players **should not** learn this purpose yet.

# Puzzle 2: The Hall of Shattered Selves

*As the first sigil settles into its steady emerald glow, the chamber grows still again. The soft blue-white light of the braziers steadies, as though waiting for the room to breathe its next breath.*

Then—the golden vapors left behind from the dissolving mirrors drift upward, swirling together in lazy spirals above the pentagram. At first they look like harmless mist.

But then they begin… **seeking.**

The vapors stretch toward each of you, dividing cleanly—one stream per person—circling you like curious serpents before pooling upon the floor directly in front of you.

The vapors thicken, condense, and rise.

A humanoid outline forms.

Then bone-white lines trace a skeleton made of thin, delicate lattices of light.

Then flesh fills in like a watercolor bleeding through paper.

Within seconds, standing before each of you is:

# **You.**

But not as you are now. A lesser version. Simpler gear. Fewer scars. Unhardened eyes. A body laced with faint golden cracks, as though held together by magic pressed too tightly.

They tilt their heads in perfect mimicry of your movements, but always **half a heartbeat late**, like reflections trapped behind time.

The second sigil on the pentagram pulses in slow golden breaths.

The chamber waits.

And your reflections… step forward.

## Puzzle Mechanics

### The Combat Encounter: "Your Weaker Self"

Each player faces a manifested copy of themselves at **Level 1**:

- Same race
- Same class
- Same subclass (if chosen at Level 1—otherwise: base class features only)
- Basic gear equivalent to a starting kit
- Hit points appropriate for a Level 1 version of that class
- Ability scores *match the player* (but HP and abilities scale to level 1)

### Combat Behavior

Your reflection:

- Always attempts to flank you if possible
- Copies your *style*, not your *specific abilities*
- Uses basic tactics, like a version of you before experience hardened your instincts
- Fights silently
- Moves exactly as you do—with a fractional delay, like a mirror struggling to keep up

### Optional Twist (Highly Recommended):

If any player attempts to attack someone else's reflection:

- That reflection mimics **that player** instead—shifting subtly at the moment of aggression as if it is "choosing" a new source to copy.

This keeps the fight personal, unsettling, and thematically sharp.

### Shatter Condition

When a reflection is reduced to 0 HP:

- It does **not** fall unconscious
- It stands perfectly still
- Its form begins to fracture like a mirror under pressure
- Cracks race through its body in straight lines of bright gold
- Then— it **shatters**, exploding into thousands of tiny golden shards
- The shards dissolve instantly into dust
- A faint **echo of the player's own voice** (level 1 tone) whispers a single word:
  - "Why?"
  - "Again?"
  - "Become."

Each reflection's death is a moment of eerie personal symbolism.

### Puzzle Completion

Once **all reflections are shattered**, the following occurs:

- The second sigil on the pentagram stops its soft golden breathing
- It ignites into a **vivid emerald green**, matching the first
- A thin beam of emerald light shoots upward from its ring to the ceiling
- The beam flickers out
- The sigil remains solid and steady

And then—

A fresh inhalation of the chamber. A subtle pull from the Soulwell above. A shift in the other sigils, as though the third is preparing to awaken.

The next challenge is ready to begin.

### Balance Suggestions

**If your party is strong:**

- Reflections roll initiative with advantage
- Grant them a once-per-fight "Surge of Instinct": A reflection can take one extra attack/action at ¼ health

**If your party is struggling:**

- Reduce reflection HP slightly
- Have reflections act second each round, always after the player they mirror
- Allow reflections to hesitate if the players attempt roleplay or introspection

## Level Progression

After this puzzle is complete, all players **advance to Level 2** before Puzzle 3 begins. Notify them: "You feel the chamber's energy flow through you. You've learned much. You are Level 2."

Healing during the brief transition: Players can spend Hit Dice to recover health before Puzzle 3 begins.

# Puzzle 3: The Hall of Elemental Remains

*As the echoes of the shattered reflections fade, the chamber settles once more into its unnatural stillness.*

The braziers flicker—once, twice—then steady again, their blue-white flames dimming as though considering what comes next.

From the spots where each reflection died, **five faint piles of golden dust remain**, sparkling like crushed starlight on the stone floor.

Then the dust… moves.

It draws inward, spiraling tightly, each pile pulling itself into a long, thin shape. Bone materializes at the center of each swirl—clean, pale white, polished like ivory.

In seconds, **five small bones lie on the floor**, each one perfectly formed. They hum faintly, resonating with quiet elemental energy.

Then, as the third sigil pulses brighter, the bones rise gently into the air.

The moment any of you reaches toward one—the bone **changes**, reshaping smoothly like melting wax drawn upward:

A wooden shaft grows from its length. Runes burn themselves along the grain. The bone fuses into a core at the top.

Each bone becomes a **wand**.

Each wand glows with a faint aura—five distinct colors, five distinct elements.

The chamber waits.

## The Five Elemental Wands

Each wand corresponds to a classic element and is imbued with a single cantrip. Only the **correct** person can use each wand.

This is where the **order from Puzzle 1** matters again.

The order the players stood in (shortest → tallest) determines the order of elemental attunement.

Assign the wands to players in that same sequence:

1. **Spirit – Light** *(soft white glow)*
2. **Earth – Mold Earth** *(dark green rumble)*
3. **Fire – Fire Bolt** *(red-orange heat shimmer)*
4. **Water – Shape Water** *(cool blue ripple)*
5. **Air – Gust** *(pale silver swirl)*

When a player picks up *their* wand:

- It warms to their touch
- Its runes flare with color
- The magic settles naturally within their hand

When a player picks up a wand that is **not theirs**:

- The wand flickers uncertainly
- Sparks jump from the bone core
- The runes sputter
- The wand becomes inert in their grip
- **The cantrip cannot be cast**

Nothing explodes. But the rejection is unmistakable.

## Puzzle Mechanics

### Step 1 – Claiming the Wands

The players must pick up the wands and determine which one aligns with which person.

A successful **Arcana (DC 12)** or **Insight (DC 14)** check reveals:

> "The wands refuse you unless you take the one meant for you."

If they cannot figure it out immediately, the wands' reactions will teach them.

### Step 2 – Casting on the Sigils

The five sigils on the pentagram (one emerald, four still dormant) each respond to their associated element.

When the correct player casts their cantrip on their corresponding sigil:

- The sigil pulses gold once
- A thin beam of gold shoots upward from its surrounding ring
- The beam flickers
- The sigil continues its golden breathing, awaiting the others

### If the order is incorrect

If a player casts:

- the wrong spell, **or**
- the right spell but not in sequence, **or**
- the right spell but with the wrong wand (if they swapped wands)

then:

- All sigils extinguish
- The third sigil resets
- The wands revert to inert bones
- The puzzle begins again

A soft whisper echoes through the chamber:

> "Order… order… order…"

### Correct Sequence

The sequence mirrors Puzzle 1's height lineup:

1. **Spirit – Light**
2. **Earth – Mold Earth**
3. **Fire – Fire Bolt**
4. **Water – Shape Water**
5. **Air – Gust**

As each sigil is correctly activated, the next one begins to glow with the soft golden breathing pulse—inviting the next spell.

### Puzzle Completion

When the final spell is cast correctly:

- The fifth sigil flashes a brilliant gold beam
- All five beams ignite in unison for a single heartbeat
- Then explode upward into the ceiling like dissipating fireworks
- Each wand dissolves into gentle golden dust
- The dust rises and vanishes into the Soulwell above
- The third sigil turns solid emerald
- The room inhales once more

The **fourth sigil** begins its slow golden pulse.

The next challenge awakens.

# Puzzle 4: The Hall of Shifting Dust

*As the last traces of golden dust from the wands drift upward and vanish into the Soulwell, the chamber falls silent again—a silence so heavy it feels like the air itself is waiting for something to happen.*

Then it does.

The blue-white flames of the braziers gutter, shrinking inward until they flicker like dying candles. The temperature drops sharply—dry, brittle, uncomfortable.

A faint *scrape* echoes across the stone floor. Then another. Then dozens more.

Above you, the ceiling—the same ceiling where the Soulwell swirls in its otherworldly haze—begins to weep grains of sand.

Not fall. Not pour.

**Weep.**

Individual grains trickle downward in thin, deliberate lines like tears. Then the trickles become streams. The streams become rivulets. And the rivulets become a rain of sand.

The sand gathers on the floor, pulling itself inward with unnatural intent.

You watch as the pile splits into two distinct shapes—

One swirling and rising like a miniature desert storm…
The other flattening into a carpet of shifting, chittering motion.

The room exhales a low, grinding whisper:

> **"Endure… or be consumed."**

The sand finishes its transformation.

## The Foes Formed From Dust

You now face two constructs born from the chamber's will:

### 1. The Sandbound Guardian

A **Small Sand Elemental**, humanoid only in silhouette—its form constantly collapsing and rebuilding itself, grains pouring like hourglass sand with each step.

### 2. The Devouring Swarm

A **Swarm of Bone-Sand Scarabs**, tiny crystalline beetles made entirely of compressed dust. Their bodies glow faintly with golden light, like sparks caught in amber.

Both creatures are **partially tethered to the Soulwell**. They leave trails of drifting sand behind them as they move.

## Puzzle Mechanics / Encounter Notes

### The Goal

Survive. There is no trick, no puzzle input—this challenge is purely **endurance and teamwork**.

### Combat Stat Blocks

See below for complete mechanical details.

**Quick Reference:**

- **Sandbound Guardian:** AC 14, HP 22, Slam attack, Sand Spray ability
- **Swarm of Scarabs:** AC 12, HP 16, Bite attack, climbing ability

### Room Interactions

During the fight:

- The braziers dim to **half-light**, creating long dramatic shadows
- The pentagram's fourth sigil pulses faster as the battle continues, responding to violence
- Footsteps echo half a beat late (a reminder of the tethered souls)
- The sand moves unnaturally:
  - It avoids the braziers
  - It snakes along cracks
  - It pulls toward the sigils whenever blood hits the floor

### Puzzle Completion

When both creatures are defeated:

- All remaining sand shudders
- Then rises in swirling spirals into the air
- The Soulwell above pulses, inhaling the final grains

The fourth sigil:

- Stops its golden breathing
- Shifts into a brilliant **emerald green**
- A soft pulse of energy sweeps across the chamber like a shockwave
- The braziers return to their steady blue-white flame

Then, as always:

The **final sigil**, the fifth, begins its gentle golden glow.

Only one trial remains.

## Level Progression

Before Puzzle 5 begins, all players **advance to Level 3**. Notify them: "You feel the Soulwell's power flow through you one final time. You are Level 3. You sense that the final trial is upon you."

# Puzzle 5: The Final Test - The Riddles of Power

*Important:* Puzzle 5 introduces the first two of five riddles. The final three riddles occur during the Kaelthys boss fight.

### Narrative Setup (DM Read-Aloud)

The chamber settles after the last of the scarabs crumbles. Only fragments remain—fine sand pooling across the floor, and thousands of tiny, iridescent shells scattered like broken stars.

For a moment, nothing moves.

Then the floor **breathes** again.

A low whisper rises from the pentagram—first a vibration, then a pulse—and the sand gathered across the stone begins to stir. The grains slide, gather, and spiral, drawn into deliberate whorls as though traced by an invisible fingertip.

The scarab shells follow, clicking faintly as they lift from the floor and drift on unseen currents. They circle the room once, then are pulled into the same spiraling pattern.

Before your eyes, the swirling mixture of sand and shell forms a moving tapestry of golden-brown script—letters made of *shifting dunes* and *gleaming fragments*. The words hang suspended, always in motion, always reforming, like sentences being written by a desert wind.

And then…

A voice.

The same mysterious, echoing presence that has threaded through the chamber since your arrival. Not loud—never loud—but inescapable, as though it curls directly into your minds.

The voice **speaks each riddle** in perfect sync with the shifting words.

And each time you reread them, the voice returns—an echo beside your thoughts—never repeating exactly the same way, never fully leaving, as if the chamber itself is repeating the words.

The **Soulwell** above gives a faint tug, as if listening with you.

This is the final test.

## The Riddle Sequence

Use these exactly as written, or swap order as desired. Voice and shifting-sand text always appear together.

### Riddle 1 – Air (Easy)

The sand swirls into a brief shape: wind currents visible in dancing dust. The voice whispers:

> "I dance without legs and sing without a tongue.
> I move freely but leave nothing behind.
> What am I?"

**Answer:** Wind or Air

**Effect:** All PCs gain **+10 ft. movement speed** for 2 rounds. The Air sigil flashes emerald green.

### Riddle 2 – Water (Moderate)

The shells shimmer, forming the shape of flowing water and rain. A faint hiss—almost like rain—accompanies the whisper:

> "I fall but never rise, I flow but never sleep.
> I can carve mountains or drown a city.
> What am I?"

**Answer:** Water or River

**Effect:** All PCs gain **resistance to Fire damage** for 2 rounds. The Water sigil flashes emerald green.

## What Happens Next

As the second riddle's answer is spoken, the sand and shells dissolve harmlessly. The second sigil flashes emerald green.

But there is no rest.

The figure of a sphinx forms one final time in the sand—but this time, it solidifies. It grows. It becomes real, standing in the pentagram before you, fully manifested.

**Kaelthys, the Shifting Riddle, has awakened.**

**Riddle 3 – Earth:** "I am solid and still, the foundation of all. Empires crumble upon me, yet I remain unchanged. What am I?"

**Riddle 4 – Fire:** "I consume but am not hunger. I warm but can also destroy. I can be born from air and water but live only briefly. What am I?"

**Riddle 5 – Spirit:** "I am what you are, yet I am not you. I know your deepest truth, yet you refuse to see me. What am I?"
**Answer:** Soul, Name, or Self

### Correct Answer Effects

- The creature who answered deals 10 radiant damage to Kaelthys with its next attack
- That creature gains 2d4 temporary HP

### Incorrect Answer Effects

- Kaelthys heals 15 HP
- All creatures that heard the incorrect answer take 4 psychic damage

### Victory Conditions

**<3 Riddles Correct:** Pyrrhic victory—Soulwell stabilizes but remains tainted, one PC suffers consequence.

## Notes on Balance

- **Melee Strikers (Role 1):** High HP and damage, lower AC depending on class
- **Ranged Strikers (Role 2):** Medium HP, high damage, medium AC
- **Spellcasters (Role 3):** Low HP, spells for versatility, low AC (watch them!)
- **Healers (Role 4):** Medium HP, healing, medium-to-high AC, good defense
- **Skill Expert (Role 5):** Medium HP, lots of skills, medium AC, inspiration support

**No role is "better" than another.** The party works best with all 5 roles represented.

## Spellcasting & Wands (Puzzle 3)

In **Puzzle 3**, characters receive magical **wands** that grant **cantrips** temporarily.

**Important:** Characters don't need to be spellcasters to use the wands. A **Barbarian can cast Fire Bolt** from the wand, for example. The wand handles the magic—just cast it like you would any other ability.

Wand cantrips available:

- Light (Spirit)
- Mold Earth (Earth)
- Fire Bolt (Fire)
- Shape Water (Water)
- Gust (Air)

# Appendices

## Reference Materials

### Quick Combat Reference

**To Hit:** d20 + Proficiency Bonus + Ability Modifier vs. AC
**Damage:** Weapon Die + Ability Modifier
**Saving Throw:** d20 + Ability Modifier vs. DC
**Healing:** Use character spells and abilities as written

### Common Ability Modifiers

- STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA 8-9: -1
- STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA 10-11: +0
- STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA 12-13: +1
- STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA 14-15: +2
- STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA 16-17: +3
- STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA 18-19: +4

### Conditions Players May Face

- **Prone:** Disadvantage on melee attack rolls, advantage on attacks against them
- **Blinded:** Disadvantage on attack rolls, attacks against them have advantage
- **Restrained:** Speed = 0, disadvantage on DEX saves and attack rolls
- **Petrified:** Incapacitated, treated as stone for resistance/vulnerabilities
- **Stunned:** Can't move or act, fails DEX and STR saves, attacks have advantage

### Spell Save DCs by Level

**Level 1:** DC 12
**Level 2:** DC 12 (scaling at higher levels)
**Level 3:** DC 12-14

### Quick Healing References

- **Healing Word/Cure Wounds:** 1d4 + WIS modifier
- **Goodberry:** 1d4 + WIS modifier per berry
- **Second Wind:** 1d10 + Fighter level
- **Lay on Hands:** Pool of 5 HP (can split across creatures)
- **Hit Dice:** Players regain 1 hit die per short rest, can spend during rest to heal
